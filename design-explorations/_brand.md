## Brand summary

Wisdom is a scripture reader. Product surfaces are **dark**: deep maroon paper, warm gold ink, cream text. Serif display type (Playfair Display) carries the reverence, Inter carries the body, Noto Sans Devanagari carries the Sanskrit/Hindi.

## Chrome adaptation (light paper for the exploration frame)

- **Paper:** #fbf5ea — cream tinted warm toward the brand's gold.
- **Ink:** #3d0c0c (maroon-deep) as darkest brand hue for headings and letter badges.
- **Accents:** #a86a12 (darker gold, AA on cream) for eyebrow chips, labels, links.
- **Line:** rgba(61, 12, 12, 0.14) — maroon-tinted hairlines.
- **Fonts:** Playfair Display 500/600 for chrome headings; Inter 400/500/600 for body; Noto Sans Devanagari kept available for Sanskrit in previews.
- **Radius:** 14-20px (matches product --r-lg 20).

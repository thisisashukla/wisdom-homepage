# 03 · SEO (homepage)

Source: Google Autocomplete (gl=in, gl=us) on about 40 seeds, plus earlier Search Console reviews (27 Sep, 10 Oct). No volume API was available, so demand labels are relative.

## Primary keyword
**bhagavad gita online**: high demand in India and globally, with navigational and transactional intent. Reader and product pages rank for it (vedabase, holy-bhagavad-gita.org, gitasupersite), not listicles, so the homepage can compete. The homepage already ranks for broad Gita terms; changing the H1 must not drop the phrase.

"Personalised gita verse" has no search demand. Use it as brand copy, not as a keyword.

## SERP gap
Every ranking page is a reader or a library. **None answers "which verse is for my situation?"**, and none shows the verse applied to one person's life. That gap is the homepage's angle.

## Title, meta, slug, H1
- Title (59 chars): `Bhagavad Gita Online: 700 Verses in Hindi, English | Wisdom`
- Meta (≤155): `Read all 700 Bhagavad Gita verses free in Sanskrit, Hindi and English. Or tell us what you're going through and get the verse that speaks to it.`
- Slug: `/`
- H1 must contain "Bhagavad Gita" and read naturally. Put the primary keyword phrase ("Bhagavad Gita online") in the first 100 words.

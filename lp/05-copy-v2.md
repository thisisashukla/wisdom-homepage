## faq
- Eyebrow: FAQ
- H2: Common questions (was "Before you start", now the which-gita eyebrow)
1. Can I read all 700 verses of the Bhagavad Gita free? Yes. All 18 chapters and 700 verses are free here, each with the Sanskrit shloka, Hindi anuvad, an English translation and commentary. No login and no ads. To read the whole text, begin at 1.1: Arjuna's despair in chapter 1 is where Krishna's teaching starts.
2. Which Bhagavad Gita translation should I read? Is this ISKCON? No, it isn't ISKCON. Read one that shows you the Sanskrit and doesn't tie you to a single sect. Our English is our own, checked against Shankara, Ramanuja and modern acharyas. For a side-by-side look at Gita Press, Prabhupada, Easwaran and others, see our comparison.
3. Is there a Bhagavad Gita verse for today? Yes. A new verse goes up every morning, with the Sanskrit, its meaning and audio. On iPhone, the Wisdom app can put it on your Lock Screen.
4. How does the 7-day plan work? Answer a few questions about you and what's going on. Wisdom picks seven verses for it, one a day, starting with the Gita and drawing on the Upanishads, Ramayana and Mahabharata. Each comes with the Sanskrit and what it means for you. Day 1 is free. On iPhone the rest of week 1 is free in the app; on Android and computers it's ₹89 a week, after you sign in with Google.
5. Is the Gita available in Hindi? Yes. Every verse has a Hindi-first page at /hi/gita with the shloka, Hindi anuvad and vistrit arth. (kept)
6. What does it cost? Reading is free. On iPhone, week 1 of a plan is free and Pro opens weeks 2 and 3. On the web, a plan is ₹89 a week. Your bank sends a notice before each charge and you can cancel from your plan. (kept)
7. Is this a substitute for therapy? No. Many people read the Gita alongside therapy. If you're thinking about harming yourself, please call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (India, free, 24×7) or your local emergency number. (kept)

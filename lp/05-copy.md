## faq
1. Can I read the Bhagavad Gita online for free? Yes. All 18 chapters and 700 verses, in Sanskrit, Hindi and English with meaning and commentary, are free on this site. No login and no ads.
2. How does the reading plan work? You tell us what's weighing on you in one tap. Wisdom picks seven verses for it from a library of 1,743 across the Gita, Ramayana, Mahabharata and Upanishads: one a day, each with the Sanskrit and what it means for you. Day 1 opens straight away and is free. On iPhone the rest of week 1 is free in the app; on Android and computers it's ₹89 a week, and you sign in with Google first.
3. Which translation do you use? Is it ISKCON? No. The Sanskrit and Hindi come from traditional sources. English is our own, checked against Shankara, Ramanuja and modern acharyas rather than one tradition. The Methodology page explains how.
4. Is the Gita available in Hindi? Yes. Every verse has a Hindi-first page at /hi/gita with the shloka, Hindi anuvad and vistrit arth.
5. Which verse should I read first? If something is on your mind, start with your verse above. If you want the whole text, begin at 1.1. Arjuna's despair in chapter 1 is where Krishna's teaching starts. The most quoted verse is 2.47.
6. What does it cost? Reading is free. On iPhone, week 1 of a plan is free and Pro opens weeks 2 and 3. On the web, a plan is ₹89 a week. Your bank sends a notice before each charge and you can cancel from your plan.
7. Is this a substitute for therapy? No. Many people read the Gita alongside therapy. If you're thinking about harming yourself, please call Tele-MANAS on 14416 (India, free, 24×7) or your local emergency number.

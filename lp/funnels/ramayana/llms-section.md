## Ramayana: Valmiki and Tulsidas, scene by scene

Film scenes from Ramayana: Part 1 checked against Valmiki's Sanskrit Ramayana and Tulsidas's Ramcharitmanas. Each page answers one question in its first paragraph, shows the original line in Devanagari or IAST with Wisdom's own English, and gives the reference (Valmiki by kanda and sarga; Manas by kand and Gita Press doha number where confirmed). Film details come only from published trailers and press reports.

- [The Ramayana behind the film](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana): what Part 1 covers in the text, by kand, with links to every page below.
- [Ramayana, in the original](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original): eleven film scenes with a verdict each (in both texts, Tulsidas only, or neither) and the lines.
- [Is the Lakshman Rekha in the original Ramayana?](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/lakshman-rekha): no line in Valmiki (Aranya Kanda 45); Tulsidas mentions it once, through Mandodari in Lanka Kand.
- [Did Ahalya become a stone in Valmiki?](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/ahalya): no; she lives unseen on air (Bala Kanda 48 to 49); the stone is Tulsidas's.
- [Sita's swayamvar and the bow](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/sita-swayamvar-bow): no assembly of kings in Valmiki (Bala Kanda 66 to 67).
- [Kaikeyi's two boons](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/kaikeyi-boons): in both; Tulsidas adds Saraswati turning Manthara's mind (Ayodhya Kand 12).
- [Kevat washes Ram's feet](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/kevat): Tulsidas only (Ayodhya Kand 100); in Valmiki Guha's men bring a boat (Ayodhya Kanda 52).
- [Bharat refuses the throne](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/bharat-refuses-the-throne): in both (Valmiki Ayodhya Kanda 79 and 112).
- [Sita into the fire before the abduction](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/sita-into-the-fire): Tulsidas only (Aranya Kand 24).
- [The golden deer](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/golden-deer): in both, told differently.
- [Sita's words to Lakshman](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/sita-words-to-lakshman): at length in Valmiki (Aranya Kanda 45), one line in Tulsidas (Aranya Kand 28).
- [Jatayu fights Ravana](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/jatayu): in both; Ram cremates him as kin (Aranya Kanda 68).
- [Shabari's berries](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/in-the-original/shabari): neither text has the tasted berries (Aranya Kanda 74; Aranya Kand 34).
- [Valmiki Ramayana vs Ramcharitmanas](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/valmiki-vs-tulsidas): language, shape, who tells it, scene differences, and what the film draws on.
- [Seven mornings after the film](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/after-the-film): four Valmiki lines, then Bhagavad Gita 2.14, 6.5 and 2.47.
- [Diwali in the Ramayana](https://wisdomquotes.in/ramayana/diwali): Ram's return to Ayodhya as Valmiki (Yuddha Kanda 125 to 128) and Tulsidas (Uttar Kand 1 to 9) tell it.
- [रामायण, मूल ग्रंथ में](https://wisdomquotes.in/hi/ramayana/in-the-original) and [दिवाली और रामायण](https://wisdomquotes.in/hi/ramayana/diwali): Hindi twins.

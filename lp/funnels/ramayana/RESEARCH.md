# Wisdom funnel: Ramayana (Valmiki) + Ramcharitmanas, timed to "Ramayana: Part 1"

*30 Oct 2026. Research + plan. Numbers carry their source; "est." marks my estimates.*

**Dates verified.** Makers confirmed 11 Oct: worldwide release **Thu 5 Nov 2026**, all premium formats incl. IMAX, in Hindi, Tamil, Telugu, Kannada, Malayalam; paid previews in India possibly 4 Nov (Outlook, 12 Oct). Earlier reports said 6 Nov international / 8 Nov India (Variety India, 8 Aug); superseded. Sony distributes on ~50,000 international screens (North America, Europe, Middle East, Australia), English trailer with AI lip-sync (Variety India, 7 Aug). Diwali is **Sun 8 Nov 2026**. Part 2: Diwali 2027.

**The fact that shapes everything:** Part 1 reportedly ends around **Sita's abduction**, with Hanuman arriving only at the climax (The Week, 1 Sep). So the film window is **Bal, Ayodhya, Aranya**. Sundarkand, the ocean, the bridge and Lanka belong to the Part 2 window (Oct to Nov 2027), which is when those pages need to be old enough to rank.

**Current footprint:** zero. GSC Jul to Oct 2026 shows one impression for "ramcharitmanas" and nothing for chaupai or Ramayana queries (GSC, wisdomquotes.in). No Ramayana routes exist in the frontend.

Evidence:
- The pull is huge and film-driven: Google Trends India has the Ramayana book topic at 2.5 to 2.7x the Gita for two weeks (brief). Indian audiences have a shared visual memory of Ramanand Sagar's TV *Ramayan*, whose 2020 rerun was reported at 77 million viewers on one day (Organiser; HuffPost India fact-checked the "world record" framing). Most people's "original" is that TV show plus the Manas, which is why "is it in Valmiki?" questions come up.
- Sentiment is primed: fans complained that the story credit went to screenwriter Shridhar Raghavan rather than Valmiki (BollywoodShaadis), and Shri Ramlila Mahasangh asked for a pre-release screening and threatened protests (Eastern Eye, 3 Aug), citing the *Adipurush* backlash. People will want "what the text says". They will not want a site mocking the film.
- Diaspora practice recurs: Durga Temple of Virginia holds Sundarkand Paath on the last Saturday of every month; children's library books frame the Ramayana as "the story of Diwali" (library catalogue).
- Girmitiya: in Fiji, satsang Ramayan mandalis use chaupai singing to pass the Manas on, the girmitiyas treated the Ramayana as the highest authority, and 20 to 23% of Fiji's schools are Sanatan Dharm schools (Swarajya, quoting Prof. Prasad). Trinidad (Global Indian, Indrani Rampersad's Ramlila revival) and Guyana (Stabroek News, Dharmic Sabha Ramlila revival) show the same mandali/Ramlila culture. Per-capita Trends (brief): Fiji 71, Mauritius 34 for Ramayana; Mauritius 46, Fiji 42 for Ramcharitmanas.
- App reality check: non-India "Resonates" is Gita 26%, Valmiki 13%, Manas 8% (brief). Use Ramayana as the way in and keep the plan mixed (Ramayana lines first, Gita by day 4).

## 3. Data audit (`sanscripts/wisdom_kb/out`)

**Ready**
- **Manas** (`rcm`, 6,149 verses): Devanagari + IAST for all; Hindi meaning for all; polished English + editorial (essence, simple/detailed meaning, insight, modern relevance) for **kands 1 to 6 at 100%**, Uttar Kand 11% (95 of 869).
- **Valmiki** (`vr`, 19,371): Devanagari for all; **IAST missing for all** (the script toggle needs a transliteration pass, e.g. indic-transliteration). Polished English: Bala 85%, Aranya 41%, **Ayodhya 2%**, Kishkindha 2%, Sundara 1%, Yuddha/Uttara 0%.
- **Curated shelf** `curated_epics.csv`: 107 publishable Ramayana lines (vr 55, rcm 52; 57 core) with display line, setting, themes and a "misuse" guard.
- **Prasangs** `library_prasangs.csv`: 76 vr + 68 rcm titled episodes with start/end ids and a one-line lesson, including the sensitive ones (`vr.p.sita_answers_suspicion`, `vr.p.sita_left_in_forest`, `vr.p.sita_returns_to_earth`) and the ocean arc both ways (`rcm.p.ocean`, `vr.p.ocean_humility_firmness`). `stories.jsonl` does not exist yet; `story_candidates.jsonl` has the 825 ranges.

**License: better than feared.** The Valmiki `en_src` is **Manmatha Nath Dutt's 1891 to 1894 translation**: "Then the foremost of Raghus addressed Ocean in stern words..." matches line 1937 of Project Gutenberg #60188 (Dutt, Yuddhakandam). Dutt is public domain. It reads archaically ("bespake Kākutstha") and has OCR noise ("carth", "Räma"), so publish our own polished English and keep Dutt as the base. The real risk is the **Manas Hindi bhavarth**: it matches the Gita Press (Hanuman Prasad Poddar) tika as mirrored on ramcharit.in. Poddar died 1971, so under India's life+60 rule it stays protected until 2032 (est.). For published pages, use our own Hindi meaning (the LLM pipeline already does this for English) and treat `hi` as reference only. Gita Press Hindi sarga titles in `vr_sargas.csv` carry the same caution.

**Blockers to fix before any Manas page**
1. **Numbering drift vs Gita Press**, which is the edition every Hindi reader holds: `rcm.5.55.doha.1` (*binay na manat*) is GP Sundar 57; `rcm.1.48.chaupai.8` (*hoihi soi*) is GP Bal 52; `rcm.1.106.chaupai.2` (*mangal bhavan*) is GP Bal 112; `rcm.7.43.chaupai.1` (*par hit*) is GP Uttar 41. The offset varies by kand (0 in Ayodhya, 2 in Sundar, 4 to 6 in Bal). Build a GP-aligned `display_ref` before labelling anything.
2. **Copy lint:** 149 Manas English strings and 579 Manas editorial blobs contain em/en dashes; about 326 hit the contrast-pattern regex. Run the existing lint at export.
3. **`rcm.5.57.chaupai.3` (*dhol ganwar*)** has an editorial that endorses the line ("True correction is a manifestation of the divine order"). It is not in `kb_bundle.json` or `library.json` (checked), so not live. Mark it `exclude` now.

**Sensitive episodes: how to handle them**
- **Shurpanakha** (vr 3.17 to 3.18; Manas `rcm.3.14` onward): tell the whole arc, including that the punishment follows her attack on Sita. No cards from the jesting verses. Never play her humiliation as comedy; character page voice stays neutral.
- **Agni Pariksha** (vr 6.115 to 6.118): centre Sita's own reply (`vr.6.116.5`, "Why do you speak to me like a common man to a common woman?") through `vr.p.sita_answers_suspicion`. Note that in the Manas the fire scene returns the real Sita after the *maya* Sita device (`rcm.6.109.chhand.1`). Never card Ram's harsh lines. Follow named traditional commentary when quoting interpretation.
- **Uttara Kanda exile:** Tulsidas's Uttar Kand does not narrate Sita's exile. Many traditional scholars treat Valmiki's Uttara Kanda as a later layer; say so with citations, then let Sita speak through `vr.p.sita_returns_to_earth`. It falls outside the film window, so publish no earlier than 2027.
- ***Dhol ganwar shudra pashu nari*:** spoken by the Ocean, a stubborn character being corrected, and not by Ram or as Tulsidas's own teaching. Exclude it from cards and plans. One explainer page is optional (big Hindi query, brand risk); if you write one, base it on scholars' readings of *tadana* and the speaker context, and get a human review.

## 5. UI/UX direction: two registers

### A. "Ramayana, in the original" (cinematic English: film-goers, diaspora, parents)
**Mood:** a night shore before dawn: dark water, one lamp, ember light on text. No deity art, no film stills.
**Tokens:** Night indigo `#0e1226` (bg) · Ember `#e8743b` (accent, verdict chips) · Moon parchment `#efe6d2` (text) · Ash blue `#8b97b5` (secondary) · Sindoor `#b3261e` (Valmiki marker) · Wisdom gold `#f5c96a` (Tulsidas marker, ties back to the parent brand).
**Type:** Cormorant Garamond 600 for display, Inter for body, Noto Serif Devanagari for original lines (IAST in Inter italic when toggled).
**Anatomy:** hero line → scene strip in film order → scene card (film moment, one sentence · Valmiki line + id · Tulsidas line + id · verdict chip · the whole arc in 3 lines · "what it asks of you") → 7-line plan → device CTA → FAQ (AI answers).
**Signature interaction: "Three lamps."** Every scene shows three diyas labelled Film / Valmiki / Tulsidas. A lit lamp means the scene is in that version; tap it and the verse unfolds beneath. Long-press exports the lamp row as a share card ("2 of 3 lamps lit").
**Copy samples:** "You watched it on the big screen. Read the line it came from." · "Ram asked the ocean for three days. Then he lifted the bow. Carry both into your week." · "Seven mornings, seven lines, before Part 2 arrives." · "Tell your kids the story the way Valmiki told it."
**Next step:** iPhone: "Put a line from Valmiki on your Lock Screen" (App Store). Android: "Get one Ramayana line a day on WhatsApp." Desktop: "Start Week 1 free" → /start?plan=ramayana.

### B. "रामचरितमानस पोथी" (devotional, Hindi Manas readers, girmitiya mandalis)
**Mood:** a pothi on red cloth at morning aarti: warm paper, kumkum, big readable Devanagari, nothing moving except the line you are on.
**Tokens:** Pothi paper `#f4ead5` (bg) · Ink `#2a1a12` (text) · Kumkum `#9e1b1b` (doha markers) · Haldi `#e0a526` (highlights) · Tulsi green `#4f6b3a` (arth) · Cloth maroon `#5a0f14` (headers, links back to Wisdom's maroon).
**Type:** Tiro Devanagari Hindi at 22 to 24px with generous line-height for matras; Mukta for UI; IAST toggle for the diaspora.
**Anatomy:** chaupai (large) → अर्थ → भाव: one line on what it asks of you today → प्रसंग (where in the story) → previous/next chaupai → "सुंदरकांड मंगलवार" strip → link to /hi/ram-shalaka → device CTA.
**Signature interaction: "पाठ मोड."** Tap anywhere to move one line forward; a counter shows chaupai/doha progress; at each doha a soft bell and the arth fold open. Resume where you left off (localStorage).
**Copy samples:** "आज की चौपाई, अर्थ के साथ।" · "पाठ करते समय अर्थ भी साथ चले।" · "हर मंगलवार सुंदरकांड की एक चौपाई, सीधे WhatsApp पर।" · "जहाँ छोड़ा था, वहीं से आगे पढ़ें।"
**Next step:** Android: WhatsApp channel (Tuesday Sundarkand line). iPhone: "आज की चौपाई Lock Screen पर" (App Store). Desktop: /start (Hindi plan email).

## 6. Traffic estimate, metrics, risks, build order

**Estimates** (all est.; base is ~380 Google clicks/mo sitewide today, no Ramayana footprint, low authority):
- **Film window (5 Nov to 31 Dec):** Google 400 to 1,500 clicks (base ~700), mostly long-tail "in Valmiki?" and chaupai queries once indexed (2 to 6 weeks). Off-Google (Reels, Reddit, WhatsApp forwards) 1,000 to 5,000 visits, high variance. At 2 to 3% list conversion that is 40 to 150 sign-ups; app trial starts 5 to 20, mostly from the diaspora on iPhone.
- **Baseline (Jan to Sep 2027):** 150 to 500 clicks/mo, led by Hindi chaupai pages and Tuesday Sundarkand searches.
- **Part 2 window (Oct to Nov 2027):** 1,500 to 6,000 clicks/mo at peak, if the Part 2 cluster and the Sundarkand page are 3+ months old by then. This is the bigger prize; Part 1 mainly builds the pages and the list.

**Metrics:** GSC clicks/impressions on `/ramayana/*` and `/hi/ramcharitmanas/*`; lamp taps per scene; share-card exports; device-CTA CTR split by device; WhatsApp joins; App Store taps with `ct=ramayana-film`; `/start?plan=ramayana` starts; in-app Resonates for vr/rcm lines among film-funnel installs vs the 13%/8% baseline.

**Risks:** (1) Indexing lag means most Part 1 traffic arrives after the peak; off-Google carries week 1. (2) Wrong refs: GP misalignment will get called out by Hindi readers within hours. (3) Hindi bhavarth copyright (Poddar, est. to 2032). (4) Tone: "film got it wrong" content invites backlash from groups already on alert; frame every card as "how the texts tell it". (5) Film IP: no stills, posters or logo; use the title only in text. (6) Editorial debt: dashes, contrast patterns, the *dhol* editorial. (7) The app is Gita-first; Ramayana lines resonate at half the Gita's rate abroad, so the plan must hand over to the Gita.

## Hanuman Chalisa in English, line by line

The full Hanuman Chalisa of Goswami Tulsidas (Awadhi), in the Gita Press reading, with Wisdom's own English and Hindi meaning for each of its 43 units: two opening dohas, forty chaupais and the closing doha. Every line has its own page with the Awadhi in Devanagari or IAST (written as sung), a word-by-word gloss, where the line happens in the Ramcharitmanas or the Valmiki Ramayana with the reference, and notes on other editions' readings, including Rambhadracharya's. Tools for reciting it: an 11-day learn-by-heart mode and a counter for 7, 11, 21 or 108 recitations or a 40-day vow.

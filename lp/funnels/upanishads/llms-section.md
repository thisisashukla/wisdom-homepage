## Upanishads

Wisdom's Upanishads section: famous lines and short complete Upanishads, each with the Sanskrit (Devanagari or IAST), a plain English translation written by Wisdom from the Sanskrit and Shankara's commentary, the source context, and how the acharyas read it (Shankara first, with Ramanuja or Madhva where they differ). Every page states its verse reference in one format, for example "Chandogya Upanishad 6.8.7". Sanskrit is reconciled against the sanskritdocuments.org editions; translations are marked as under human review.

- [The Upanishads](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads): what they are, how many (108, ten principal), which Veda each belongs to, where to start.
- [उपनिषद क्या है](https://wisdomquotes.in/hi/upanishad): Hindi explainer, upanishad kya hai and kitne hain, the ten principal Upanishads.
- [Tat tvam asi](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/tat-tvam-asi): "you are that", Chandogya Upanishad 6.8.7, Uddalaka to Shvetaketu; Devanagari forms for writing it out.
- [Satyameva jayate](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/satyameva-jayate): "truth alone wins", Mundaka Upanishad 3.1.6, full verse; India's national motto. Hindi: [सत्यमेव जयते](https://wisdomquotes.in/hi/upanishads/satyameva-jayate).
- [Asato ma sadgamaya](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/asato-ma-sadgamaya): Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.3.28 and the Upanishad's own explanation of the three lines. Hindi: [असतो मा सद्गमय](https://wisdomquotes.in/hi/upanishads/asato-ma-sadgamaya).
- [Uttishthata jagrata](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/uttishthata-jagrata): "arise, awake", Katha Upanishad 1.3.14, Yama to Nachiketa.
- [Ishavasyam idam sarvam](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/ishavasyam-idam-sarvam): Isha Upanishad 1, with Shankara's and Vedanta Desika's readings.
- [Aham brahmasmi](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/aham-brahmasmi): "I am Brahman", Brihadaranyaka Upanishad 1.4.10, full passage.
- [Purnamadah purnamidam](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/purnamadah-purnamidam): the Isha invocation, also Brihadaranyaka 5.1.1.
- [Om saha navavatu](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/sahana-vavatu): the Krishna Yajur Veda shanti mantra that opens the Katha and Taittiriya.
- [Matru devo bhava](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/matru-devo-bhava): Taittiriya Upanishad 1.11.2, the teacher's parting advice.
- [Bhidyate hridaya-granthih](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/bhidyate-hridaya-granthih): Mundaka Upanishad 2.2.8, the knot of the heart.
- [Which Upanishad to read first](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/which-to-read-first): a reading order and an honest guide to English translations (Gambhirananda, Nikhilananda, Easwaran, Olivelle, Gita Press).
- [Isha Upanishad, full text](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/isha): all 18 verses and the invocation, anchors per verse (#v1 to #v18).
- [Mandukya Upanishad, full text](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/mandukya): all 12 verses, Om and the four states, turiya.
- [Katha Upanishad, full text](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/katha): all 119 verses laid out as the dialogue between Nachiketa and Yama, anchors per verse (#v1-2-18 for 1.2.18); notes where the acharyas differ; bridges to Gita 2.19, 2.20, 8.11 and 15.1.
- [The four mahavakyas](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/mahavakyas): prajnanam brahma, aham brahmasmi, tat tvam asi, ayam atma brahma, with sources and Vedas.
- [Who am I: a 7-verse plan](https://wisdomquotes.in/upanishads/who-am-i): Kena 1.1, Mundaka 3.1.1, Katha 1.2.18, Isha 6, Mandukya 2, Chandogya 6.8.7, Brihadaranyaka 1.4.10, one a day.

## Shri Ram Shalaka Prashnavali (श्रीराम शलाका प्रश्नावली)

The Shri Ram Shalaka Prashnavali is a 15 by 15 grid of 225 syllables that tradition links to Goswami Tulsidas, printed with Gita Press, Gorakhpur editions of the Ramcharitmanas. You hold a question, touch a square, and read every ninth syllable; the 25 syllables spell one of nine chaupais of the Manas, which is the answer. Wisdom serves the traditional grid as a crawlable HTML table, a free guided version of the ritual that works like a small app (type a question or tap an example, touch a syllable, watch every ninth syllable gather into the chaupai, then the verdict seal; one free try, then a free Google sign-in; three questions a day; the question never leaves the phone; a shareable answer card), and a page for each of the nine chaupais with the full chaupai, its meaning, its scene in the Manas, the traditional sign and a related Gita verse. Meanings and scenes are Wisdom's own wording, written in line with the Gita Press commentary tradition.

## Mahabharata

Pages on the Mahabharata built from its Sanskrit: books 3, 5, 12, 13 and 14 from a southern-recension text, with Wisdom's own English read through Nilakantha's commentary. Every line shows its Sanskrit, who says it and a verse reference in the form "MBh 5.146.23" (our text's numbering, which can differ from the Pune critical edition). The Bhagavad Gita is chapters 23 to 40 of the Bhishma Parva in the critical edition (25 to 42 in the vulgate).

- [Where the Gita sits in the Mahabharata](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/where-is-the-gita): Bhishma Parva, who narrates it (Sanjaya to Dhritarashtra), when, and Arjuna asking to hear it again after the war (MBh 14.17).
- [महाभारत में गीता कहाँ है](https://wisdomquotes.in/hi/mahabharata/where-is-the-gita): the same page in Hindi.
- [Mahabharata hub](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata): the 18 books in brief, an interactive Kuru family tree with the Day 1 armies, and FAQs (authorship, length, the 100 Kauravas, Ashwatthama).
- [Yaksha prashna](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/yaksha-prashna): every question and answer at the lake (MBh 3.314) in Sanskrit and English, Yudhishthira's choice of Nakula, and a quiz.
- [Vidura Niti](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/vidura-niti): Vidura's counsel to Dhritarashtra (MBh 5.33 to 5.40), seven verses on anger, and the lines left out and why.
- [Mahabharata quotes](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/quotes): 25 real lines with Sanskrit, speaker and verse number, including "yato dharmas tato jayaḥ" (MBh 5.39.9, Dhritarashtra).
- [Karna](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/characters/karna): why he stayed with Duryodhana (MBh 5.141.12) and his promise to Kunti (MBh 5.146.23).
- [Draupadi](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/characters/draupadi): her own words: the boon that freed her husbands (MBh 5.81.28) and her case for action (MBh 3.32.3).
- [Vidura](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/characters/vidura): Dhritarashtra's half-brother and counsellor.
- [Kunti](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/characters/kunti): the Vidula story she sends Yudhishthira (MBh 5.133) and telling Karna he is her son (MBh 5.145.2).
- [Bhishma](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/characters/bhishma): the vow and why he kept it (MBh 5.147).
- [Yudhishthira](https://wisdomquotes.in/mahabharata/characters/yudhishthira): the Yaksha's questions and his choice (MBh 3.314.135).

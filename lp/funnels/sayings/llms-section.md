## Sayings: Vivekananda, Kabir and Chanakya, checked against their sources

Quote pages for three teachers whose lines are shared most in India, each line traced to its source. Every Swami Vivekananda line is matched against his Complete Works (vols 1 to 8, Mayavati Memorial Edition) and shows its volume and work; popular lines he never said are marked as such. Kabir's dohe carry where each one is found (Kabir Granthavali, Kavita-Kaumudi 1917, or the sung tradition), and Chanakya Niti verses carry their chapter and verse in the Sanskrit. Meanings are Wisdom's own.

# 00 · Brief: wisdomquotes.in homepage

## Product
Wisdom: "Bhagavad Gita, tuned to your life. One verse a day."
- **Website (free):** all 700 Gita verses in Sanskrit, Hindi and English with meaning and commentary. New: pick how you feel and what it is about, and get one verse chosen for it, with the Sanskrit and what it means for you.
- **iPhone app (v3, in review; store still shows 2.1.1):** a 7-day plan around your situation (see it → steady it → act on it → a story), read aloud, with the shloka woven in. Home Screen and Lock Screen widgets. Week 1 free; Pro opens weeks 2–3 (pattern underneath, who you are beyond it).
- **Web plan (Android/desktop):** the same 7-day plan built from your own words, ₹89/week via Razorpay. Google sign-in is required to see a verse off-iPhone.

## Job of the homepage
1. Funnel to the app (iPhone) and the web plan (everyone else).
2. Keep ranking for "bhagavad gita online / in hindi / in english" and grow traffic about 10×.

## Proof we actually have (nothing else may be claimed)
- 700 verses with Sanskrit, Hindi and English. Commentary drafted from Shankara, Ramanuja and modern acharyas, reviewed by a person.
- A library of 1,743 verses across 14 texts and 462 stories (from the backend's /recommend/hook response).
- Real Wisdom output for 2.47 from three test notes (/recommend/depth, 19 Oct 2026).
- Three real App Store reviews (AbhimanyuDasgupta, rahuliitb16, Dibbbuuu), April 2026. No rating count to show; the store displays none.
- Mixpanel, 60 days: 257 real homepage visitors. 61% click something, 13% scroll a quarter of the page, 216 site visitors played shloka audio.
- Search: 81% of clicks from India, 78% mobile. Problem-intent queries have Google Autocomplete demand (overthinking, death of a loved one, failure, stress, anger).

## Constraints
- Keep the current visual system: maroon night, diya gold, Playfair and Inter, the Krishna hero art.
- Every verse shown carries its Sanskrit or Indian-language original.
- Read verses through Hindu scholarship; no colonial or Gandhi readings; never invent scripture.
- No fake ratings, testimonials or numbers.

holy-bhagavad-gita.org (Mukundananda), vedabase (ISKCON), Gita Press, GitaGPT/ChatGPT, generic quote and wallpaper apps, YouTube pravachans.

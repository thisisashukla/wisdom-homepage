# 04 · Structure (homepage)

## Awareness
Traffic is mixed: Google searches for "bhagavad gita online" (product-aware for the Gita, unaware of Wisdom), Instagram and Reddit links (problem-aware, already in the knot), and internal verse pages (reading). The hero has to work for problem-aware visitors without losing readers. So: lead with the problem and the finder, and keep "read online" one line away.

Behaviour constraint (Mixpanel): only 13% of visitors scroll past a quarter of the page. The hero must carry the whole argument. Everything below it is for the minority who keep reading, and for SEO.

## Deletion test
- **Proof bar** (old): nothing is left unanswered if removed; the hero eyebrow carries "700 verses · Sanskrit, Hindi, English". **Deleted.**
- **"Three lives" (invented lines)**: replaced by three-notes with real output. Without it, the AI-slop objection has no answer.
- **three-notes** (19 Oct, Revision 4): about 250 words of strangers' notes about one verse. It argued "tuned" instead of showing it, and had no action. Replaced by plan-preview: the visitor taps their own situation and sees the real week, which is the thing the button creates.
- **seven-days** (Revision 5): its step list repeated what plan-preview shows, and it showed iPhone details to Android visitors. Replaced by devices: one switch preset to the visitor's device, one price, three facts, one button.
- **"Wisdom for iPhone" three-card block**: merged into seven-days. Its only job was the plan and the offer.
- **Blog row**: kept inside library; it carries the best-translation query, which is the top non-India entry page.
- **sources**: removing it leaves the ISKCON and AI objections unanswered. **Kept.**

## 1. Facts and calls to check before launch

1. **Where the Gita sits: the brief's numbering is the vulgate, not the critical edition.** The brief says "Bhishma Parva, chapters 25 to 42 in the critical-edition numbering". Ganguli (vulgate, Nilakantha's text) starts the Gita at Bhishma Parva Section XXV and ends it at XLII (checked in Gutenberg #15475). The Pune critical edition has the Gita at 6.23 to 6.40 (so Gita 1.1 = MBh 6.23.1). Every page says "23 to 40 in the critical edition, 25 to 42 in the vulgate". Please confirm against a BORI copy.
2. **Our Sanskrit is not the critical edition.** Adhyaya counts in the corpus (Vana 315, Udyoga 196, Shanti 375, Anushasana 274, Ashvamedhika 118) match a southern-recension printed text of the Kumbhakonam type. Pages say "a southern-recension text whose numbering runs close to the Kumbhakonam edition". Ganguli alignment holds for Vana and Udyoga (Ganguli Vana CCCXI = our 3.314; Udyoga XXXIII = 5.33; CXXXIII = 5.133; CXLVII = 5.147). Please confirm the edition from the source of `processed_mahabharat_shloka`.
3. **Yaksha prashna in the critical edition**: the FAQ says "in the Pune critical edition the questions are in chapter 297". Verify (our 3.313 to 3.315).
4. **Speaker of mbh.12.221.48** ("na jātu kāmaḥ"): it sits in Panchashikha's teaching to Janaka in our southern text and is absent from Ganguli's matching section; it is better known as Yayati's (Adi Parva). Shown on the quotes page as Panchashikha's, via Bhishma. Drop it if in doubt.
5. **Speaker of mbh.5.36.5, 5.36.12, 5.36.13**: these are the swan's (Atreya's) words to the Sadhyas, quoted by Vidura; labelled so. 5.36.5 recurs as our 12.305.16.
6. **mbh.5.147.20 and 5.147.34 to 35** are Bhishma's words to Duryodhana as Krishna reports them to the Pandavas (Ganguli Udyoga CXLVII confirms). Labelled "Bhishma to Duryodhana" with that context.
7. **mbh.5.39.9** ("yato dharmas tato jayaḥ") is Dhritarashtra's line, refusing Vidura (Ganguli XXXIX confirms). The quotes page says it is the Supreme Court of India's motto. Check.
8. **Showcase ids not published**: mbh.5.81.34 (narration of Draupadi taking her hair in her left hand). We show her own words, 5.81.36, instead, without a card. mbh.5.81.24 (her account of being dragged into the hall) is shown on her page with no share actions and no card.

LINE: Whoever runs others down in public only puts their own faults on display.

MEANING: Bhishma, lying on his bed of arrows, tells Yudhishthira how to handle a fool's public slander. The fool who tears someone down in a crowd only lifts the hood of his own faults for everyone to see. Their words are not a verdict on you. They are a mirror they hold up to themselves.

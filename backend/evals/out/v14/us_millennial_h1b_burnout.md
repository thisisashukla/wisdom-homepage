## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 1.29–30
LINE: Shaking limbs, a dry mouth, a racing body: the Gita opens with a panic attack, not a hero.
TRANSLATION: My limbs give way, my mouth is parched, my body trembles and my hair stands on end.
MEANING: Arjuna stands in his chariot between two armies, and sees his own kin ready to kill one another. His body shakes, his mouth goes dry, his hair stands on end. The Gita opens with a man freezing, not a hero stepping forward. It says that even the strongest can collapse, and the teaching begins inside that collapse. The verse proposes what is already happening: the body is telling the truth about the fear, and it is allowed to feel it.
FOR YOU: Your body knows what your mind is trying to outrun. That dry mouth and the trembling limbs are not a failure. They are the truth of what is happening. Let them be there without fighting them.

LINE: Grief wears away your steadiness and your judgement. There is no enemy like it.
TRANSLATION: Grief destroys steadiness, grief destroys learning, grief destroys everything. There is no enemy like grief.
MEANING: Kausalya, Rama's mother, speaks to Dasharatha after she has lashed out at him over Rama's exile. She tells him that grief wears away the steadiness and the judgement of a person. She says that there is no enemy like grief, because it destroys everything it touches. She offers this not as an excuse, but as a warning about what sorrow can do to those we love.

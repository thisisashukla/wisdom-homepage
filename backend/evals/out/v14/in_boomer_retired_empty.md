## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 9.22
LINE: Turn to me with your whole heart, and I will carry what you lack and guard what you have.
TRANSLATION: To those who think of me alone and worship me, ever devoted, I bring what they lack and preserve what they have.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. He is drawing a line between worship done for rewards and worship done without desire. He tells Arjuna that those who think of him alone, with whole devotion, will find their lacking met and their having preserved. The verse offers trust as a way to live, promising that the need to gather and defend will not press so hard on the heart.

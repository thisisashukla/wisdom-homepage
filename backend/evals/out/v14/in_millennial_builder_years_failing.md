## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna that the work which fits your own nature is the right work. He says that doing your own duty, even if it is flawed and you stumble along the way, is better than taking on someone else's path and doing it perfectly. The point is that your own work, with all its natural imperfections, is a kind of worship in itself. You do not need to be flawless to be on the right track.
FOR YOU: You have been building apps for years. The doubt you feel is not about the code, but about the question you are asking. Let the work you are doing now be the path, even if it feels imperfect.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidula's counsel
LINE: Every result is uncertain. Some who try succeed and some don't. Those who never try never do.
TRANSLATION: The result of every action is uncertain. Knowing this, some people act and succeed, some do not. But those who never act never succeed at all.
MEANING: Queen Vidula finds her son Sanjaya lying defeated after losing his kingdom. She tells him that the result of every action is uncertain, and some who try succeed while others do not. But those who never try never succeed at all. She is waking him from despair, showing him that the outcome is never guaranteed, yet standing still guarantees nothing.
FOR YOU: The years of side projects are not the failure. The failure is the quiet belief that you could know the exact outcome before you move. You are waiting for certainty in a place where it does not exist. The doubt is not about your skill.

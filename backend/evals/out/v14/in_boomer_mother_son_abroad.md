LINE: In grief, ask: where did I come from, where am I going, who am I? Let that steady you.

LINE: 'Out of sight, out of mind' is true only of the ungrateful. Real love survives distance.
TRANSLATION: Love lasts while someone is in sight; out of sight, affection fades. But that is the way of the ungrateful. Ram will not let it fade.
MEANING: Sita sits alone, surrounded by guards who watch her every hour. She is afraid that Ram has forgotten her, that the years of separation have worn down his love. It asks you to trust that a bond does not vanish just because it is not seen.
FOR YOU: The love you have for your son is not a debt to be paid back. It is real, and it survives the distance between you. Let it stand as it is.

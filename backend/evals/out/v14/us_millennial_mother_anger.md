## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 11.24
LINE: Seeing something too big to take in, my heart trembles and I can't find steadiness or calm.
TRANSLATION: Seeing you touching the sky, blazing with many colours, mouths wide open, with huge fiery eyes, my inner self trembles, Vishnu, and I find neither courage nor peace.
MEANING: Arjuna has just seen Krishna's cosmic form, and the sight is too vast to hold. He tells Krishna that his heart trembles and he cannot find any steadiness or calm. The verse says that when a person faces something immense and all-consuming, it shakes them to the core. It is a human reaction, not a failure. Arjuna is overwhelmed by the scale of what he is seeing, and his body reacts before his mind can catch up.
FOR YOU: You are trembling after that snap, and the fear that follows is just the body reacting to something vast and unnamed. You are not broken. You are simply meeting the size of what is happening inside you right now.

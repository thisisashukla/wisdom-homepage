LINE: A full house, then one person, then many, then no one. Time plays dice with our lives.
TRANSLATION: Where there were many in a house, one remains; where there was one, many come, and in the end not even one. So Time, rolling night and day like a pair of dice, plays on the board of the world with living beings as its pieces.

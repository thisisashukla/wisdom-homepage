## Day 4 [steady] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Today's effort and yesterday's keep wrestling. Whoever stays calm through it wins.
TRANSLATION: Present effort overcomes the past, and the past can overpower the present. It is always a contest of human striving, and the one who stays calm wins.
MEANING: Vasishtha speaks to the young Rama, who is sitting in a cave, heavy with the weight of his father’s death and the loss of his kingdom.

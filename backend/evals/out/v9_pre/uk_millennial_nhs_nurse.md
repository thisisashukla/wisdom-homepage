LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on a battlefield, where Arjuna is frozen by the fear of doing his own duty imperfectly. The point is that you do the work that fits your own nature, and when you offer it, you do not incur any wrong. The work is not about perfection, but about honest fit with who you are.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 18.16
LINE: If you think you alone made it happen, or alone made it fail, you're not seeing the whole picture.
TRANSLATION: That being so, whoever, through an untrained understanding, sees himself alone as the doer does not truly see.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield after listing the five causes behind any action: the setting, the doer, the instruments, the effort, and providence. Arjuna is about to act alone, believing the entire burden rests on his shoulders. Krishna stops him. He says that whoever looks at their own life through an untrained mind, thinking they alone made it succeed or fail, is not truly seeing. You are one cause among five. You own your part. The rest belongs to the whole.
FOR YOU: The picture your mother-in-law holds is not the whole story. You are one cause of this tension, not the whole cause. See the other forces at work so you are not crushed by a single, false verdict.

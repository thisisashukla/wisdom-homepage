## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Same person, same mind, same voice. Lose money, and people suddenly treat you as someone else.
TRANSLATION: The same faculties, unimpaired; the same name; the same unclouded mind; the same speech. Yet a man deprived of the warmth of wealth becomes, in a moment, someone else. How strange!
MEANING: Bhartrihari speaks to the reader about the strange way the world shifts its gaze. The same mind, the same voice, the same unclouded faculties remain, yet a man who loses his wealth becomes someone else to those around him. The verse observes this change without saying it is just, and it points to the fact that the worth is in the person, not the purse.
FOR YOU: Your father sees a different person today than the one who runs the business. The worth you carry is not the one he is handing back. The room he enters is not the room that holds your full name.

LINE: Turn a torch upside down and its flame still rises. Steadiness survives humiliation.
TRANSLATION: Even when harassed, the steadfast cannot be robbed of their steadiness. Hold a torch upside down: its flame never points downward.

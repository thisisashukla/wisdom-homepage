## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna stands before Krishna and admits his mind will not settle. It is restless, turbulent, powerful and stubborn, as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna hears this and agrees. The mind is indeed difficult to restrain, he says, but through practice and letting go it can be trained. The truth is not that the mind must be conquered by force, but that it can be steadied by returning to the present, again and again.

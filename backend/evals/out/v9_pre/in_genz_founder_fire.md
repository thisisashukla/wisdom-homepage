## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.48
LINE: Don't abandon work that suits you just because it has flaws. Every undertaking has some, like fire has smoke.
TRANSLATION: One should not give up the work suited to one's nature, Kaunteya, even if it has faults; for all undertakings are clouded by faults, as fire is by smoke.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the field, telling him that no work is perfect. He says you could not walk away from the work that fits who you are just because it is hard or has flaws. Every path has smoke, like a fire that gives off smoke while it burns. The work itself is the offering, even when it is messy.
FOR YOU: You quit your job and the brand is yours now. That electric feeling is the work, not the prize. Let the building be the reward, even on the days it feels messy.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
FOR YOU: The first hard day will come. When it does, do not let the lack of income become the prize. Let the work itself be the reward. That is how you stay free.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 2.40
LINE: No sincere effort here is ever wasted. Even a little of it shields you from great fear.
TRANSLATION: On this path no effort is lost and no harm results. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that no sincere effort on this path is ever lost. Even a small amount of this practice protects you from great fear. Do the work fully, and give up clinging to the outcome.
FOR YOU: You are building your brand without a safety net. Trust that every honest hour you put in is not wasted. That steady effort is what will hold you up when the fear comes.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari tells the reader that the steadfast do not leave the right path no matter what people say. They praise or blame, money comes or goes, death can come today or later, but the steady keep walking. The idea is that setbacks rarely last for those who keep going on the right path.
FOR YOU: No honest effort on the right path is ever wasted; even a little of it protects you and carries you forward.

## Day 5 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
FOR YOU: Decide what you will do today, then begin. Do not stall halfway or waste time worrying. Keep yourself steady by doing one small, concrete thing that moves your brand forward.

## Day 6 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Don't announce your plans; let people see your results.
TRANSLATION: Don't talk about what you are going to do; let people see only what you have done. That way your plans for duty, pleasure and gain are never exposed.
MEANING: Vidura tells Dhritarashtra to keep his plans private and let his results speak. When a person announces their intentions, their ideas for gain and duty are exposed before they are even tested.
FOR YOU: Keep the building of your brand to yourself for now. Let the results you produce each day be the only thing that speaks.

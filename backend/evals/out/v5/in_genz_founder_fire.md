LINE: Your work is yours to do; the results are not yours to hold. Don't let that make you stop.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that the disciplined person, who gives up the fruit of action, finds lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the payoff, is bound. The disciplined act without letting the world soak into them.
FOR YOU: Yesterday you let the work go. Today you see the pattern. The hard days will come. Do not let the silence of the inbox or the empty wallet soak into you. Do the work, and let it go.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari speaks to the reader from a place of steadiness. He says that praise and blame, money and loss, even death itself, are all passing things. The steadfast person feels none of these shifts. They do not step off the path of what is right. The verse proposes that when you are building something that is truly yours, the outside noise does not move you. The steady do not leave the path no matter what people say.
FOR YOU: Your brand is new and the praise is still coming. Hold on to the feeling of being alive and keep doing the work. The steady do not step off the path of what is right, even when the numbers are quiet.

## Day 5 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
MEANING: King Dhritarashtra lies awake before the peace talks, and Vidura sits with him. Vidura tells him that the wise person makes a clear choice and then acts. They do not stop halfway, they do not waste the hours, and they keep themselves steady. The fool hesitates and burns their energy. The wise person moves with a calm hand and a steady mind, and that is what brings real happiness.
FOR YOU: Your brand is yours to build. Set one small task for today and let your hands do it without stopping halfway. Keep your attention on the work, not on the hours.

## Day 6 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Don't announce your plans; let people see your results.
TRANSLATION: Don't talk about what you are going to do; let people see only what you have done. That way your plans for duty, pleasure and gain are never exposed.
MEANING: Vidura speaks to the king again, this time about the company a person keeps. He says that a wise person keeps their plans to themselves and lets their results do the talking. If you announce what you are going to do, your plans become exposed before they are ready, and people may try to use them against you. Let your work speak first.
FOR YOU: Keep your next step to yourself. Let your work speak before you tell anyone what you are building.

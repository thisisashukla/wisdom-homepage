## Day 2 [diagnose] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: 'Done this, doing this, must do that.' Caught in endless lists, the mind never gets a moment's rest.
TRANSLATION: Carried along on endless great waves of tasks, the mind never gets a moment's rest; its depths dance with worry. 'This I've done, this I'm doing, this I'll do': so the bird of the mind, tangled in a net of reckonings, faints.
MEANING: Bhasa tells Vilasa that the mind is carried along on endless great waves of tasks, never getting a moment's rest. It faints in a net of reckonings, caught between what has been done, what is being done, and what must be done.
FOR YOU: The lists you carry are not the problem. They are the symptom of a mind left to itself for too long. The exhaustion comes from the running, not the work itself.

## Day 3 [steady] Ashtavakra Gita 18.58
LINE: A restless mind is agitated even doing nothing; a skilled one stays calm doing everything.
TRANSLATION: The fool is restless even when doing nothing; the skilful one stays calm even when doing everything that needs doing.
MEANING: Ashtavakra speaks to Janaka, who has already given up his kingdom to seek peace. Ashtavakra tells him that the fool is restless even when doing nothing, while the skilled one stays calm even when doing everything that needs doing. The contrast is not between action and rest, but between two ways of holding life. The untrained mind chases results and never arrives. The trained mind rests inside the doing, because it knows its own nature is not the doing itself.
FOR YOU: Your mind is still running the list of what you owe, even when the phone is down and the room is quiet. The doing is real, but the restless part is not. See it as a habit, not a command. The calm you need is not in the next transfer or the next call.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 6.19
LINE: Like a lamp in a windless place that doesn't flicker: that's a settled mind.
TRANSLATION: As a lamp in a windless place does not flicker: that is the image used for the yogi of controlled mind practising union with the self.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna how to meditate, how to bring the mind home. He says the mind is like a lamp in a windless place. It does not flicker. It just holds its light. This is what a mind that is trained in moderation looks like. It does not run after every thought. It just stays where it is put, steady and calm.
FOR YOU: After the phone calls, let your mind be like that lamp. You do not need to earn this quiet. Just let it stay. Come back to it, again and again, without forcing it.

## Day 6 [act] Bhagavad Gita 12.8
LINE: Rest your mind in me, settle your thinking in me, and you will live in me.

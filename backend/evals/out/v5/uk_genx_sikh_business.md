## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, where Arjuna is frozen by doubt. Krishna tells him to act according to his own nature rather than imitating others. To be true to yourself, even with flaws, is safer than living a borrowed life. Borrowed roles carry fear, while your own path, however imperfect, brings a steadier footing.
FOR YOU: Your father has lived his life, and now he is trying to live yours. That borrowed role is not yours to fill. Your own path, the one you have built at work and at home, is the only one that belongs to you. Let that truth stand.

LINE: Be on your own side. Lift yourself up, don't push yourself down. You can be your own friend.
TRANSLATION: Raise yourself by your own self; do not let yourself sink. For the self alone is the friend of the self, and the self alone is the enemy of the self.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, where Arjuna is paralyzed by doubt and grief. ## Day 5 [act] Maha Upanishad 6.64-65
LINE: Do the work in front of you, speak gently, read people's hearts, and you won't sink in the world.
TRANSLATION: Always doing the work that comes, seeing friend and foe alike, free of wanting and not-wanting, such a one neither grieves nor craves. Saying what is agreeable to all, speaking gently when asked, knowing the intent of others' hearts, such a one does not sink in the world.
MEANING: Ribhu is teaching Nidagha how a person who is truly free moves through the world. They do the work that is right in front of them. They look at friend and enemy with the same clear eyes. They are not pulled around by wanting things or pushing them away. Because of this, they do not grieve over what is lost, and they do not crave what is not theirs. When they speak, they say what everyone can agree with, and they speak gently. They also take time to read the true intent of people's hearts.
FOR YOU: Yesterday's verse named the grip. Today, let it loosen. When you sit down to do your accounting work, let your hands move with the same steady focus Ribhu speaks of. Do the work in front of you without letting the noise in your home pull your mind under.

TRANSLATION: Speech that causes no distress, that is truthful, pleasant and beneficial, and the regular study of sacred texts: this is called austerity of speech.

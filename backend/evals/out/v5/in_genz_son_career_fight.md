## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: In the Niti Shatakam, Bhartrihari speaks to the reader from a place of quiet certainty. He has seen how quickly the world changes its mind and how unstable its rewards are. He tells us to let the crowd praise or blame, to let money come or go, and to accept that life can end at any moment. His point is simple. The person who is steady does not step off the right path. They keep going no matter what happens outside them.
FOR YOU: You are holding your truth while your father shouts. Let the fight happen. Let him praise or blame. Your worth is in the work you do, not in his words. Keep going to your classes. Let his noise stay outside the door.

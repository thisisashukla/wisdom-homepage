## Day 2 [diagnose] Ashtavakra Gita 11.5
LINE: Suffering is born of worry, nothing else. Free of worry, you are happy and at peace.
TRANSLATION: One who knows for certain that suffering here is born of worry and nothing else is free of it, happy, at peace, and unattached everywhere.
MEANING: Ashtavakra speaks to Janaka about what makes a person truly calm. He says that suffering is born of worry and nothing else. The wise person knows that things arise and pass by nature, that fortune and misfortune come in their time, and that suffering is born of worry alone. Knowing for certain that worry is the source of pain, and that nothing else, loosens the grip. You are left happy, at peace, and unattached everywhere.

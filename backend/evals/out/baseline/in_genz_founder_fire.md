## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.48
LINE: Don't abandon work that suits you just because it has flaws. Every undertaking has some, like fire has smoke.
TRANSLATION: One should not give up the work suited to one's nature, Kaunteya, even if it has faults; for all undertakings are clouded by faults, as fire is by smoke.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna after describing the duties of each kind of person by their nature. He tells him not to abandon the work that fits him just because it has flaws. Every undertaking is clouded by faults, he says, just as fire is clouded by smoke. The work he has taken up has its own smoke, its own imperfections. But that is the nature of any real fire. The point is to do this work, with all its flaws, as an offering.
FOR YOU: Your brand has smoke. There will be days when the numbers do not come, when the work feels messy, when the path is unclear. This is the smoke of a real fire, not a reason to go back to the old job. Keep tending the flames.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna about the disciplined person who gives up the fruit of action. By letting go of the payoff, this person attains lasting peace. The undisciplined person, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, remains bound. The disciplined person acts in the world without letting it soak into them, like a lotus leaf untouched by water.
FOR YOU: You are building something that may not pay for a long time. The discipline is to work without clinging to the first sale, the first follower, the first check. Let the results be water on a leaf. The work is its own reward until it is not.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari writes that the steady person does not step off the right path. Let the wise blame or praise. Let fortune come or go. Let death come today or ages hence. The steadfast do not move a step from their path. The gods kept churning the ocean past jewels and poison until they found nectar. The steady do not leave the path whatever people say.
FOR YOU: There will be a morning when the work stops being exciting. There will be silence where there was once a spark. This is the moment to hold the path. The steady person does not step off. You are the one who keeps going.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 2.40
LINE: No sincere effort here is ever wasted. Even a little of it shields you from great fear.
TRANSLATION: On this path no effort is lost and no harm results. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that on this path of dedicated action, no sincere effort is ever wasted and no harm results. Even a little of this practice shields you from great fear. He says to work wholeheartedly and give up clinging to outcomes, staying steady whether success comes or not.
FOR YOU: When you check your phone and see no income, let that go. Focus on the one piece of work you do today, and trust that this effort itself is the shield.

## Day 5 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
MEANING: Vidura tells the sleepless King Dhritarashtra that a wise person decides calmly and then begins. They do not stop halfway, waste time, or lose control. They keep themselves steady and bring the work to its end.
FOR YOU: Pick one small task for your brand today and finish it. Do not let the phone or the noise of the day pull you into starting and stopping.

## Day 6 [act] Valmiki Ramayana · Kishkindha Kanda
LINE: Keep working and the results will come. Sink into despair and it is hard to get back up.
TRANSLATION: Those who keep working surely see the fruit of their work. But once you sink into deep despair, you cannot rise again.
MEANING: Angada tells the exhausted monkeys that those who keep working will surely see the fruit of their labor. But once you sink into deep despair, it is hard to rise again. He urges them to move forward and not give up.
FOR YOU: You are building something that is truly yours. When the first hard days arrive, do not let a quiet morning or a small setback pull you into the belief that you have failed. Keep working, and the results will come.

## Day 7 [story] Building the bridge
LINE: The army stood on the shore with no boats, until a quiet monkey said he knew how to build a bridge.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, where Arjuna is frozen by doubt and grief. He tells him that every being follows their own nature. It is better to walk your own path, even if you stumble, than to wear another's clothes with perfect fit. Borrowed lives are dangerous because they do not fit the soul. The path of another is full of fear, because it asks you to betray who you are.
FOR YOU: Your father wants an MBA because he can see the shape of it. But he cannot see the shape of you. Today, let your own path be the one that exists in your mind. Let it be imperfect, let it be messy. It is still yours, and it is still real.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, telling him to stop worrying about how his duty compares to others and to focus on his own path. He says that doing the work that fits your own nature, even if it is imperfect, is better than performing another person's duty perfectly. The point is that your own path, with all its flaws, is the right one for you to offer.

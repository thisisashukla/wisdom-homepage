## Day 1 · Listen (script)
[scene] Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. He has been describing his divine manifestations. Now he turns to those who follow him with devotion. He tells Arjuna that those whose minds rest on the divine, and who live their lives in that light, find joy simply by talking with one another about what they love. And Krishna replies:
[sanskrit] मच्चित्ता मद्गतप्राणा बोधयन्तः परस्परम्, कथयन्तश्च मां नित्यं तुष्यन्ति च रमन्ति च.
[line] In plain words: Their minds on me, they talk of me with each other, and find joy and contentment in it.
[unpack] This is not about gathering a crowd. It is about the quality of the mind. When the mind is fixed on what is lasting, conversation becomes a kind of light. People do not need to perform or impress. They simply share what is true for them, and in that sharing, they find contentment. This is easier said than done, because the mind naturally drifts to what is missing, to the silence of an empty house.

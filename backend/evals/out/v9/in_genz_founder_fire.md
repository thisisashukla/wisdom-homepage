## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.44
LINE: Past practice carries you forward, even when you don't feel like it.
TRANSLATION: By that former practice he is carried on, even against his will. Even one who merely wishes to know yoga goes beyond the ritual word.
MEANING: Arjuna asks Krishna what happens to someone who tries to hold onto this path and then falls. Krishna tells him that no sincere effort is ever lost. The practice you have already done carries you forward, even when you do not feel like it, even when you are moving against your own will. You do not start from zero again. The next sincere attempt picks up exactly where the last one left off.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 9.18
LINE: I am your goal, your support, your witness, your home, your shelter and your friend.
TRANSLATION: I am the goal, the sustainer, the master, the witness, the home, the refuge and the friend; the origin and the dissolution, the ground, the treasure-house and the undying seed.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that he is the one who receives every offering, the ground beneath every foot, the shelter for every being. He is the witness to every act and the final home to which every soul returns. This is not a promise of ease, but the truth that nothing is left without its resting place. The verse asks that you see the quiet house not as an end, but as a space that is held.
FOR YOU: The house is very quiet now, and the children call on Sundays. Let this verse tell you that the quiet is not empty. It is a shelter, a witness, and a home for the love you still hold. Rest in that truth today.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Mahabharata · Shanti Parva · Shanti: Consolation of Yudhishthira
LINE: In grief, ask: where did I come from, where am I going, who am I? Let that steady you.
TRANSLATION: 'Where was I before? Where will I go? Who am I, really, and why am I here? Why should I grieve, and for what?' Ask these, and steady the mind.
MEANING: King Janaka asks the sage Ashma how to bear the loss of family and wealth. Ashma answers that pain comes from what we lose and that meetings end. He asks Janaka to steady his mind with three questions: where was I before, where will I go, and who am I really. This is not to dismiss grief, but to hold it steady by knowing the self that remains when everything else passes.
FOR YOU: The house is quiet now, and the absence is loud. Let the grief be what it is. Then ask yourself where this quiet comes from and where it goes. Let the self that asks these questions be your steady ground in the silence.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 2.13
LINE: Childhood, youth, old age: the body keeps changing. The wise aren't thrown by the next change.
TRANSLATION: Just as the embodied self passes through childhood, youth and old age in this body, so it passes on to another body. The steady person is not bewildered by this.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, who is trembling with grief over the faces he must face. Krishna tells him that the soul inside the body is eternal, passing through childhood, youth, and old age like stages of a single life, and then moving into another body. The wise person sees this constant change and is not thrown off balance by the next shift. The self within you does not die when the body does, and it cannot be taken from you.
FOR YOU: The quiet in your house is a change in the body, not in you. When the grief rises, sit with it and know that the part of you that loved so fully is the same part that remains. You cannot lose who you are.

FOR YOU: Shabari waited for Ram with fruit she had gathered every day, though he had never come. Her teachers had gone, and the world did not see her. But her waiting was a kind of preparing, and when he arrived, she could welcome him with everything she had kept. Your search is not wasted.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that doing your own duty, even if you stumble, is better than copying someone else perfectly. The path you choose must come from inside you. When you try to live a borrowed life, you are always afraid of being found out. Your own work may have flaws, but it is yours. The borrowed one will always feel like a test you never agreed to take.
FOR YOU: The marriage questions are a borrowed test. Your night shifts and your salary are your own duty. They may feel imperfect, but they are yours. Let that be enough for today.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 2.63
LINE: Anger clouds judgement, clouded judgement loses memory, lost memory wrecks reason, and then you fall.
TRANSLATION: From anger comes delusion; from delusion, confusion of memory; from confusion of memory, the ruin of understanding; and with understanding ruined, a person is lost.
MEANING: Krishna is answering Arjuna. Arjuna wants to know what a steady mind looks like in the middle of a crisis. Krishna tells him it is not the absence of trouble. It is a mind that does not let one thing pull it apart. When you let anger take root, it clouds what you know. Then you forget the truth of the situation. Then your reason breaks down, and you fall. The path to steadiness is guarding your attention. It is choosing what you give your energy to, because that is where the trouble begins.
FOR YOU: The test is not real. The test is the conflict between two duties you both honour. The shift pays the bills, and the calls ask for a life you do not have yet. That is not a failure. It is a pull. See it as two right things, not a wrong choice.

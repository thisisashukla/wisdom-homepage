## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.7
LINE: I'm confused about what's right. Tell me plainly what's best; I'm ready to learn.
TRANSLATION: My nature is stricken by weakness and my mind is confused about what is right. I ask you: tell me for certain what is best for me. I am your student; I have come to you, teach me.
MEANING: Arjuna has collapsed on the battlefield, his body shaking with grief and confusion. He cannot see a clear path forward through the horror before him, and he tells Krishna that his mind is clouded and his strength has failed him. He stops trying to decide what is right on his own and asks Krishna to teach him plainly what he should do. The verse shows that clarity does not come from pushing through alone, but from admitting when your own mind cannot see the way.
FOR YOU: You manage your team at work with a calm that looks like control. At home, that same self gets blocked, and the anger comes out. The shame you feel is not a sign that you are failing.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 2.62
LINE: Dwell on something and you get attached; attachment becomes wanting, and blocked wanting turns into anger.
TRANSLATION: When a person dwells on objects, attachment to them is born. From attachment comes desire, and from desire, anger.
MEANING: Arjuna asks Krishna how a steady mind looks. Krishna tells him that when a person dwells on objects, attachment is born. From attachment comes desire, and from blocked desire, anger. The anger is not the real enemy. It is the result of a wanting that was allowed to take root by where the mind was placed.
FOR YOU: The snap at the spilled milk is not the truth of who you are.

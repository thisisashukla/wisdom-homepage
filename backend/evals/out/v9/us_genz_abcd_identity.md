## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna that it is better to do your own work, even if it is flawed, than to do someone else's work perfectly. Borrowed lives carry fear. This is not about staying in a role your family assigned you. It is about acting from your own nature, free of ego and the need to please. The verse asks you to trust the life you are already living.
FOR YOU: You are caught between your parents' picture of your future and the life you are actually living. You do not have to choose between them and your parents. Trust the path that is already yours.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 18.16
LINE: If you think you alone made it happen, or alone made it fail, you're not seeing the whole picture.
TRANSLATION: That being so, whoever, through an untrained understanding, sees himself alone as the doer does not truly see.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna after explaining that five forces shape every action: the setting, the doer, the tools, the effort, and providence. Krishna warns that if a person looks at an outcome and believes they alone made it happen, or that they alone caused it to fail, they are missing the full picture. The verse asks us to see ourselves as one cause among five, owning our part without claiming the entire result.
FOR YOU: You are not the only reason your parents are pushing this marriage. Their fear and their image of you are causes too. Own your part in choosing your life, but do not carry the whole weight of their disappointment.

LINE: You are pure awareness, wider than any worry. Don't let your mind shrink into pettiness.

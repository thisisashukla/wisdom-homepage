## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.26
LINE: Free of clinging and boasting, full of resolve and enthusiasm, unchanged by success or failure: the best kind of doer.
TRANSLATION: The doer who is free from attachment, does not speak of himself, is full of steadiness and enthusiasm, and unmoved by success or failure, is called sattvic.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. He is laying out the difference between a clear mind and a restless one. He describes the person who acts with steadiness. This person does not cling to results. They do not boast about what they are doing. They move forward with quiet resolve and enthusiasm. When success comes, they do not swell with pride. When failure comes, they do not collapse. They remain unchanged by either outcome.
FOR YOU: You have signed your first paying customer. The high is real, but the ground you want is the resolve beneath it. You are choosing to stay unchanged by the next high or low. That is your work now.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari writes that the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right. He lists the noise of the world, the coming and going of money, and even the certainty of death. None of it makes them step off. Setbacks rarely last for those who keep going. The verse asks you to hold the right path steady even when the world around you is pulling in every direction.
FOR YOU: Your phone will ring with praise and with doubt. Let the money come and go as it does. Keep your hand on the work that is yours to build. The path you chose is the only one that leads to the next ten customers.

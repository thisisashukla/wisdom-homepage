## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna while they are on the battlefield, telling them that it is better to follow your own path, even if you stumble along the way, than to wear someone else's shoes and walk their road perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous because they do not fit who you are. The fear lives in the borrowed role, not in your own.
FOR YOU: You are serving flat whites and your mates are at Goldman and Deloitte. That picture is a borrowed life you are measuring yourself against. Your own path is the one you are on right now, and it is the only one that can hold you.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Ramcharitmanas · Uttar Kand
LINE: Envy is an itch. Burning at another's happiness is a wasting disease of the mind.
TRANSLATION: Possessiveness is ringworm and envy is the itch; the swings of elation and dejection are throat ailments. The burning you feel at another's happiness is consumption, and malice and a crooked mind are leprosy.
MEANING: Kakbhushundi speaks to Garuda about the mind's hidden illnesses. He lists envy as a deep itch, a burning sickness that wastes a person from the inside. The joy of another's success becomes a throat ailment, a disease of consumption. He is naming the exact fever that is making you feel small and stuck.
FOR YOU: When you open LinkedIn and see where your friends are, you feel that burn. That itch is envy, and it is eating your energy. You are not failing at your flat whites. Your mind is sick with a comparison it cannot name.

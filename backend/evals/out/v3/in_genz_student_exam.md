LINE: Wherever the restless mind wanders, gently bring it back. Again and again.
TRANSLATION: Wherever the restless, unsteady mind wanders, from there one should draw it back and bring it under the control of the self.
MEANING: Krishna is teaching Arjuna how to sit with his mind.

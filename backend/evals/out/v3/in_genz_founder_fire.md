## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.48
LINE: Don't abandon work that suits you just because it has flaws. Every undertaking has some, like fire has smoke.
TRANSLATION: One should not give up the work suited to one's nature, Kaunteya, even if it has faults; for all undertakings are clouded by faults, as fire is by smoke.
FOR YOU: The brand you are building is yours and it fits you. The hard days will come, and they will look like smoke. Do not mistake the smoke for a reason to stop the fire.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Chhandogya Upanishad 3.14.1
LINE: A person is made of resolve. As you resolve in this life, so you become. So resolve well.
TRANSLATION: All this is Brahman; from it all arises, into it all dissolves, in it all breathes; so one should meditate on it calmly. A person is made of resolve. As one's resolve is in this world, so one becomes on departing. So let one form a resolve.
MEANING: Shandilya tells the reader that a person is made of resolve. As your resolve is in this world, so you become on departing. The point is to form a resolve well, because it shapes what you become.
FOR YOU: Yesterday's verse named the fire. Today, see what will test it. You are watching the prize and the job you left. Do not let either one dim the resolve that is making you.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari says that the steady do not step off the right path, no matter what people say or what happens. The point is that setbacks rarely last for those who keep going on the right path.
FOR YOU: You will feel the pull to look back and the push to look forward. Let them praise or blame. Your part is to keep moving forward on the path you chose. The steady do not move a step from it.

## Day 4 [steady] Mahabharata · Vana Parva · Vana: Draupadi and Yudhishthira
LINE: Don't despair because effort hasn't paid off yet. Every attempt can go either way.
TRANSLATION: 'I keep working and still don't succeed' is no reason to despair. Every effort can end two ways, in success or not; only by not acting is the outcome certain.
FOR YOU: No honest effort on the right path is ever wasted; even a little of it protects you and carries you forward.

## Day 5 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
FOR YOU: Rise and act; do your work fully and well each day, let the result go, and keep at it when no one is watching.

## Day 6 [act] Bhagavad Gita 2.47
LINE: Your work is yours to do; the results are not yours to hold. Don't let that make you stop.
TRANSLATION: Your right is to the work alone, never to its fruits. Do not let the fruit be your motive, and do not cling to doing nothing.
FOR YOU: Act as an offering, with full effort and no claim on the fruit, and stay the same whether the work is praised or ignored.

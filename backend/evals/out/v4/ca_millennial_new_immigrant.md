## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. Arjuna has laid down his weapons, overwhelmed by grief and confusion over his duty. Krishna tells him that it is better to walk one's own path, even if it is flawed and imperfect, than to perform another's role perfectly. Doing the work that fits your own nature, with all its flaws, means you incur no spiritual wrong. The work itself becomes an offering when it comes from your own true self.
FOR YOU: You are driving through the night in a country that does not know your name. That path is yours, imperfect as it feels right now. Do the work of this life, the driving and the building, as your own offering.

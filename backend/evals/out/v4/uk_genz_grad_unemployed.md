LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna stands on the battlefield and tells Krishna that the mind is wild, stubborn and as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna hears this and does not tell him to force it still. ## Day 2 [diagnose] Ramcharitmanas · Uttar Kand
LINE: Envy is an itch. Burning at another's happiness is a wasting disease of the mind.
TRANSLATION: Possessiveness is ringworm and envy is the itch; the swings of elation and dejection are throat ailments. The burning you feel at another's happiness is consumption, and malice and a crooked mind are leprosy.
MEANING: Kakbhushundi tells Garuda that envy is an itch and the burning you feel at another's success is a wasting disease of the mind. He names the symptoms clearly so they can be seen. The point is to know your mind's diseases by name, not to hide them. When you name the itch, you stop scratching it blindly.
FOR YOU: That burning in your chest when you see a friend's new job is not a sign of your failure. It is an itch, and it has a name. Naming it is the first step toward letting it pass instead of letting it run your day.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.67
LINE: When the mind chases a wandering sense, it is swept off like a boat in the wind.
TRANSLATION: When the mind runs after the roving senses, it carries away one's understanding, as the wind carries a boat away on the water.
MEANING: Arjuna asks Krishna what a steady person looks like. Krishna says they are content within, unshaken by sorrow, and master of their senses. Then he warns that when the mind chases after what the senses wander toward, it is swept away like a boat in the wind, carrying understanding with it.
FOR YOU: You are not the mind; thoughts come and go before the silent witness that watches them and is never disturbed.

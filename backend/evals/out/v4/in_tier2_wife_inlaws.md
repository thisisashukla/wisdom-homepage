## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.5
LINE: Be on your own side. Lift yourself up, don't push yourself down. You can be your own friend.
TRANSLATION: Raise yourself by your own self; do not let yourself sink. For the self alone is the friend of the self, and the self alone is the enemy of the self.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. Arjuna is broken and cannot see a way forward. Krishna tells him that he must be his own support. The self can be its own greatest ally, or its own worst enemy. The choice is whether to lift the self up or push it down into shame and defeat. This is about staying with yourself when no one else will.
FOR YOU: What you are cannot be cut smaller by what they see; your worth was never theirs to grant, and standing in your own truth is itself the duty.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna continues with Arjuna. He says that borrowing a life that is not yours is dangerous. It is better to walk your own path, even with mistakes, than to perform someone else's role perfectly. Living for another's approval or standard creates constant fear because it is not rooted in your own truth.
FOR YOU: Yesterday you were told to step back from your work. Today, stay on your own path. It is better to build your practice imperfectly on your own terms than to live a borrowed life that looks right to others but erases you.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna stands in the middle of the battlefield and tells Krishna that the mind is restless, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. He says it is as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna agrees. He says the mind is hard to hold, but practice and letting go train it.

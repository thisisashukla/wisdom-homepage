## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that the disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined person, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound. The point is to be in the mess without letting it soak in. You are building your own thing, and the highs and lows will come. Let them pass through you like water off a lotus leaf.
FOR YOU: When the morning feels flat and the work is just work, let go of the payoff. Do the next small thing without needing the high to hold you.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari says to the reader: let the worldly-wise blame or praise. Let fortune come or go. Let death come today or ages hence. The steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right. The point is that setbacks rarely last for those who keep going on the right path. The gods kept churning the ocean past jewels and poison until they found nectar. A struck ball bounces back.
FOR YOU: No honest effort on the right path is ever wasted; even a little of it protects you and carries you forward.

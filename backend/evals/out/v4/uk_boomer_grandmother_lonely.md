## Day 1 [hook] Kavitavali
LINE: Abandoned as a child, he poured out his hunger just once, and was held.
TRANSLATION: My mother and father brought me into the world and left me; fate wrote nothing good on my forehead. Lowly, scorned, timid, I hungered like a dog for scraps. Then Tulsi heard of Ram's nature and, just once, showed the Lord his empty stomach. Such a master is Raghunath that he held nothing back, in this world or the next.
MEANING: Tulsidas speaks from his own early years, when he was left alone and grew up begging for scraps. He says that his parents brought him into the world and then left him, and fate wrote nothing good on his forehead. He describes himself as lowly, scorned, timid, and hungry. Then he tells of a single moment when he showed his empty stomach to the Lord. In return, Raghunath held nothing back and gave him everything in this world and the next. The verse proposes that it is never too late to be held.
FOR YOU: You belong more than you feel right now; the people and the practice that hold you are still within reach.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 4.22
LINE: Content with what comes, beyond envy, steady in success and failure, you stay free even while working.
TRANSLATION: Content with whatever comes unasked, beyond the pairs of opposites, free from envy, the same in success and failure, even when acting he is not bound.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna how the wise move through the world. He says that a steady person is content with what comes, free from envy, and the same in success and failure.

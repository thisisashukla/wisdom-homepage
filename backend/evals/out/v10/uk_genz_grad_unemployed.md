## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, knowing Arjuna is frozen by shame and the weight of his own family. Krishna tells him that it is better to walk your own path, even if you stumble, than to wear another's armor perfectly. A life lived in imitation of others carries a hidden fear, because it is not your own. The verse asks you to trust your own nature, even when it feels rough around the edges.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.37
LINE: What happens to someone who believes, tries, slips, and doesn't make it to the end?
TRANSLATION: Arjuna said: One who has faith but does not strive enough, whose mind falls away from yoga before reaching perfection, what end does he come to, Krishna?
MEANING: Arjuna stands in the middle of the battlefield, his body heavy with doubt. They have been trying to build these apps for years, and the failures have worn them down to the bone. They ask Krishna what becomes of someone who believes, tries, slips, and does not make it to the end. Krishna answers that no sincere effort is ever wasted, and what you have already done carries forward into your next attempt.
FOR YOU: The years of trying have not vanished. They are still there, waiting under the surface. You do not need to find a new reason to begin, only to see the ground you have already covered.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidula's counsel
LINE: Every result is uncertain. Some who try succeed and some don't. Those who never try never do.
TRANSLATION: The result of every action is uncertain. Knowing this, some people act and succeed, some do not. But those who never act never succeed at all.
MEANING: Queen Vidula finds her son Sanjaya lying defeated after losing his kingdom. She tells him that the result of every action is uncertain. Some who try succeed and some do not. But those who never try never succeed at all. The failure of the apps is not the end. The loop of trying without a clear next step is the real failure. The days slipping by are the proof of the loop, not of your inability.
FOR YOU: You have tried many times to build these apps. The results were uncertain, and you did not succeed. But you never stopped trying. That refusal to stop is the one thing that has not failed, even when the apps did.

LINE: A good mind doesn't leave what is right, though a million obstacles come.

MEANING: Angad stands in Ravana's court, his foot planted on the ground. Ravana has insulted him, but Angad holds his place. His foot does not move, and the pride of the enemy drains away. A steady mind does not leave what is right, though a million obstacles come. It is not about winning the argument right now. FOR YOU: You are tired of the failure, but the app is not the real thing you are holding. The real thing is the part of you that still knows building is right. Let that part of you stand still today, while the doubt and the noise move around it. Just keep the foot planted.

## Day 5 [act] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Want it, work for it step by step, and you will get there, unless you quit halfway.
TRANSLATION: Whoever wants something and works for it step by step will surely get it, unless they turn back halfway.
MEANING: Vasishtha is speaking to the young Rama, who has just seen his father die and fears a world without justice. The teacher tells him that fate is not a fixed thing handed down from above. It is only the fruit of past effort. A person who wants something and works for it step by step will surely get it, unless they turn back halfway. The past is not a prison. Today's effort is the seed of tomorrow. Do not grieve what was never in your hands. The only real failure is to stop halfway.
FOR YOU: The loop of trying without a clear next step is what has you stuck. Break it today by choosing one small, concrete step for one of your apps and finishing it. Let the rest wait. Your past effort shaped this moment, but your present effort shapes what comes next.

## Day 6 [act] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Daily practice and clear thinking make the mind clear, slowly, the way a mango ripens.
TRANSLATION: Through daily practice and discernment the mind soon becomes clear, just as a mango slowly ripens into its sweetness.

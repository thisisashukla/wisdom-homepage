## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.5
LINE: Be on your own side. Lift yourself up, don't push yourself down. You can be your own friend.
TRANSLATION: Raise yourself by your own self; do not let yourself sink. For the self alone is the friend of the self, and the self alone is the enemy of the self.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna that the power to rise or fall rests entirely with them. They are the one who has to live with the results, so they must choose to treat themselves as a friend rather than an enemy. It is not about standing alone in the world, but about refusing to abandon oneself in the moment of defeat.
FOR YOU: What you are cannot be cut smaller by what they see; your worth was never theirs to grant, and standing in your own truth is itself the duty.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.47
LINE: Your work is yours to do; the results are not yours to hold. Don't let that make you stop.

MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. The war is about to begin, and Arjuna is frozen by the weight of what will come of his actions. Krishna tells him to focus on the act itself. Do the work fully and completely. The outcome belongs to forces beyond anyone's grasp. Let the result go. This is the yoga of action: to act without letting the future own your mind right now.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Arjuna stands on a battlefield, unsure of his place in the world, and Krishna tells him to trust his own nature. He says that doing the work that truly fits you is better than performing another person's role perfectly. It is not about staying in a fixed box. It is about doing your own work as an honest offering. There is no wrong in it, even if it is messy.
FOR YOU: You are fighting every night because your father wants an MBA for you. He does not see the films you dream of. This verse says your own path, walked imperfectly, is still better than his perfect plan. You do not have to be perfect to be worthy.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna speaks to Krishna in the middle of the battlefield, and he tells him the mind is as hard to hold as the wind. He says it is restless, stormy, and stubborn, and he feels he cannot control it. Krishna hears this and says the mind is hard to hold, but practice and letting go can train it.

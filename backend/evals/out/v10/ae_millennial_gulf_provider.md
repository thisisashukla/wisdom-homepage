## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is restless and hard to hold. He says it is like trying to catch the wind. Krishna agrees. He says the mind is stubborn and strong, but it can be trained through practice and by letting go of what pulls it apart.

TRANSLATION: Carried along on endless great waves of tasks, the mind never gets a moment's rest; its depths dance with worry. 'This I've done, this I'm doing, this I'll do': so the bird of the mind, tangled in a net of reckonings, faints.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 5.20
LINE: Don't get carried away by good news or crushed by bad. Keep your understanding steady.
TRANSLATION: One should not rejoice on getting what is pleasant nor be troubled on getting what is unpleasant. Steady in understanding and undeluded, the knower of Brahman abides in Brahman.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. He is telling him that the one who truly knows the self does not get carried away by good news or crushed by bad. They see pleasure and pain as things that come and go. They do not let these passing events shake their understanding. They stay steady and find their happiness within. This is the way to abide in what is real.
FOR YOU: Your mind is pulled by the eleven years of money sent, the father's surgery, and the wife's wish to return. You do not have to let each of these events crush you. You can hold them all without letting any one of them break your steady understanding.

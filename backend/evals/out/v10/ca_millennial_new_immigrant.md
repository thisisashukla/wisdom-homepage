LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. Arjuna is paralyzed by grief and confusion about what is right. Krishna tells him that it is better to follow your own nature and do the work that is yours, even if you do it imperfectly, than to take on the duty of another and do it perfectly. The work born from your own being, done without attachment, carries no blame. You are being asked to trust your own path, flaws and all.

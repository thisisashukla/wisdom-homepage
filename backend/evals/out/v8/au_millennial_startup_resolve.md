LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, where everything is chaos and Arjuna is frozen by what comes next. He says that the disciplined person acts without holding onto the result. They are like a lotus leaf in water. The result cannot stain them. The undisciplined person chases the payoff and becomes chained to it. The idea is to be inside the mess without letting it soak in.

LINE: Do the work that's yours. Doing something beats doing nothing; even living needs action.

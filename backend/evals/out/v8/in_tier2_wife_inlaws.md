## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, reminding him that the work he was born to do is his alone to carry. He says that doing your own work, even if it is not done perfectly, is better than doing someone else's work perfectly. The imperfections belong to the path, not to the person walking it, so the one who acts according to their own nature does no wrong.
FOR YOU: Your CA practice is the work born of your own nature. It is not theirs to cancel. The imperfections of building it from zero do not make it less yours, and you incur no wrong by doing it.

LINE: Like a bird on a string, the mind flies everywhere, finds no rest, and returns to the breath.
TRANSLATION: As a bird tied by a string flies in every direction and, finding no resting place anywhere else, settles down at the very place it is tied, so, dear one, the mind flies in every direction and, finding no resting place anywhere else, settles down on the breath; for the mind is tied to the breath.
MEANING: Uddalaka asks Shvetaketu how a person knows they are asleep. The son says they do not know anything. The father says the same self is there, resting. The mind flies everywhere at night and finds no resting place. It comes back to the breath. The mind is tied to the breath.

LINE: Don't get carried away by good news or crushed by bad. Keep your understanding steady.
TRANSLATION: One should not rejoice on getting what is pleasant nor be troubled on getting what is unpleasant. Steady in understanding and undeluded, the knower of Brahman abides in Brahman.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna about a person who sees clearly, one who is not tossed about by what happens. The world brings pleasant things and unpleasant things, and both arrive and leave. The one who knows what is real does not get carried away by joy when things go well, nor does the mind break when things go badly.

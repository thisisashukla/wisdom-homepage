## Day 2 [diagnose] Mahabharata · Vana Parva · Vana: Draupadi and Yudhishthira
LINE: Never look down on yourself. Nothing good grows from self-contempt.
TRANSLATION: A person should never look down on himself. The one who despises himself never finds real prosperity.
MEANING: Draupadi speaks to Yudhishthira while they are in the forest after losing their kingdom. She is angry that he has accepted their ruin and waits for fate to fix it. She tells him that a person must never look down on themselves, because the one who despises themselves never finds real prosperity. She argues that doing right is its own reason, but people must still act as if their own effort is what matters.
FOR YOU: You are preparing so much because you believe you could earn your place every single day. The truth is that you were promoted because of what you already did. Let your work speak for itself today and stop preparing for a disaster that has not arrived.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: A ball knocked down bounces back up. For good people, setbacks rarely last.
TRANSLATION: A ball struck down by the hand flies right back up. As a rule, the misfortunes of those who live rightly do not last.
MEANING: Bhartrihari writes that a ball knocked down by the hand flies right back up. He says that as a rule, the misfortunes of those who live rightly do not last. The image is of steady effort. FOR YOU: The fear you carry does not last. It comes in waves, and it passes. Today, trust that the work you do is solid ground. The over-preparing will not stop the fear, but the work itself will steady you.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Mahabharata · Shanti Parva · Bhishma, Rajadharma
LINE: What was ends, and what wasn't begins. Grief has no power to change it.
TRANSLATION: What was, ceases to be; what was not, comes to be. Grief has no power to change this. Why then should anyone grieve?
MEANING: The sage Kalakavrikshiya speaks to Prince Kshemadarshi, who has lost his kingdom and wealth. He tells the prince that what has already ended stays ended, and what has not yet begun is simply beginning. Grief cannot turn back the clock and bring back what was. The sage asks why anyone should grieve when the past is fixed. The verse proposes that wanting back what is gone is a dead end, and that a steadier mind looks for what is actually in front of it and makes peace with what others have.
FOR YOU: The life you built in Hyderabad is finished, and this new one in Canada is just beginning. The grief for what ended is not useless, but it cannot rebuild the past. The present you are in right now is not ruined by the move itself.

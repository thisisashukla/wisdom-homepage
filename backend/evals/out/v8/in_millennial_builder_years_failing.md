## Day 1 [hook] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Some never start for fear of obstacles; some quit when obstacles hit. The best keep going.
TRANSLATION: The lowest do not begin at all, for fear of obstacles. The middling begin, but stop when obstacles strike. The best, struck by obstacles again and again, do not give up what they have begun.
MEANING: Bhartrihari is describing the three kinds of people who face obstacles. Some never begin, afraid of what might go wrong. Some begin, but stop the moment a problem appears. The best, however, are struck again and again, and still they do not let go of what they started.
FOR YOU: You have been building apps for years and the result is nothing. You do not know if you are disciplined or stubborn. This verse names that stuckness. It says some quit when obstacles hit, but the best keep going.

LINE: Idleness feels good and ends in misery. Effort feels hard and ends in happiness.
TRANSLATION: Idleness feels pleasant but ends in sorrow; capable effort feels hard but gives rise to happiness. Good fortune and prosperity live with the capable, not with the idle.
MEANING: A brahmin speaks to King Senajit, who is crushed by grief, and says that idleness feels good but ends in misery, while capable effort feels hard but gives rise to happiness. Good fortune lives with the capable, not with the idle.
FOR YOU: The years of building your apps have felt hard and unrewarded, and you are tired of the doubt. But the pattern beneath this is not failure. It is the cycle of delay that has run its course. You are not the one who stopped knowing what to do.

## Day 6 [act] Valmiki Ramayana · Sundara Kanda
LINE: Refusing to lose heart is the root of success. Look again where you have not yet looked.
TRANSLATION: Not losing heart is the root of success; not losing heart is the highest happiness. I will search again, in the places I have not yet searched.
MEANING: Hanuman stands in Lanka, having searched all night without finding Sita. Despair is close, the cost of failure heavy, but he tells himself to refuse losing heart. He says he will search again, in the places he has not yet looked. The verse proposes that when you have looked everywhere, the next step is simply to look again. Steadiness does not come first. It comes from doing, from acting again in the very place you have already tried.
FOR YOU: You have looked at your apps, your code, your plan, and found nothing. Today, look again where you have not yet looked. Open the project folder, not to fix it, but to see one file you have not opened in months. That is the small act that starts.

## Day 1 [hook] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidula's counsel
LINE: Blaze like a torch, even for a moment. Don't just smoulder to stay alive.
TRANSLATION: Like a torch of tinduka wood, blaze, even if only for a moment. Do not smoulder without flame like a fire of husks, just to stay alive.
FOR YOU: No honest effort on the right path is ever wasted; even a little of it protects you and carries you forward.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Effort brings results, sooner or later, depending on time and place. That timing is what we call fate.
TRANSLATION: Results come from effort, sooner or later, depending on place and time. That arrival of the fruit, early or late, is what people call fate.
MEANING: Vasishtha speaks to Rama while he is weighed down by sorrow. The young prince has lost his kingdom and feels the future is closed. Vasishtha tells him that the fruit of action comes from effort itself. The timing of that fruit, whether it arrives early or late, is what people call fate. He explains that your past effort made today what it is, and your present effort will make tomorrow. Do not grieve for what was never in your hands to control.
FOR YOU: Rise and act; do your work fully and well each day, let the result go, and keep at it when no one is watching.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari addresses the reader from a section on steadfastness. He says let people praise or blame you. Let money come or go. Let death come today or ages from now. The steady person does not step off the right path. Setbacks do not last long for those who keep going on the right path.
FOR YOU: The days of your new work will be hard and the money will not be there yet. Let the praise or blame come. Let the money come or go. The only thing that matters is that you do not step off the path you chose. Keep going.

## Day 4 [steady] Mahabharata · Vana Parva · Vana: Draupadi and Yudhishthira
LINE: Don't despair because effort hasn't paid off yet. Every attempt can go either way.
TRANSLATION: 'I keep working and still don't succeed' is no reason to despair. Every effort can end two ways, in success or not; only by not acting is the outcome certain.
MEANING: In the forest, Draupadi tells Yudhishthira that waiting for fate is no strategy. She says that crying over unrewarded effort is useless. Every attempt can end in success or failure, and only by refusing to act is the outcome guaranteed. She is saying that you could act, because the only way to fail for sure is to stop trying.
FOR YOU: The fire you feel is not a feeling to wait for. It is a choice to keep working while the money is not there. Do the next small thing for the work itself, not for the reward that has not arrived.

## Day 5 [act] Bhagavad Gita 2.47
LINE: Your work is yours to do; the results are not yours to hold. Don't let that make you stop.
TRANSLATION: Your right is to the work alone, never to its fruits. Do not let the fruit be your motive, and do not cling to doing nothing.
MEANING: On the battlefield, Krishna tells Arjuna to act fully but let go of owning the result. Your right is to the work alone, never to its fruits. Do not let the outcome be your motive, and do not cling to doing nothing. Care, plan, and aim well, but do not let the result own you.
FOR YOU: You cannot control the money that comes or goes. You can only control the next small thing you do today. Do that one thing fully, and let go of needing it to prove anything right now.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield. He tells him to look at the work that is his by nature. He says it is better to walk your own path, even if you stumble along the way, than to wear another person's shoes and do their job perfectly. The point is simple. Do the work that fits your own nature, with all its flaws, and offer it up. No one can claim you did wrong by being true to who you are.
FOR YOU: You built this practice from nothing. That makes it your own path, not a borrowed role. When your mother-in-law says you will leave it, she is asking you to wear her shoes. You are not wrong for being true to the work you built.

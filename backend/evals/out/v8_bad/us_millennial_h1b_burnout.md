## Day 1 [hook] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: 'Done this, doing this, must do that.' Caught in endless lists, the mind never gets a moment's rest.
TRANSLATION: Carried along on endless great waves of tasks, the mind never gets a moment's rest; its depths dance with worry. 'This I've done, this I'm doing, this I'll do': so the bird of the mind, tangled in a net of reckonings, faints.
MEANING: Bhasa and Vilasa are two old friends who meet after many years apart, both having spent their lives in hard work and austerity. Bhasa asks Vilasa if he has found any peace. Vilasa answers honestly that there is no peace while the mind is caught in endless tasks. He says the mind is carried along on great waves of duties, always thinking 'this I have done, this I am doing, this I must do.' a bird tangled in a net of reckonings, it faints from never having a moment of rest.
FOR YOU: Your mind is caught in that same endless net. It says you could check the work message at two in the morning or the visa will be lost. The visa is real. But the mind that will not let you sleep is not.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Ashtavakra Gita 11.5
LINE: Suffering is born of worry, nothing else. Free of worry, you are happy and at peace.
TRANSLATION: One who knows for certain that suffering here is born of worry and nothing else is free of it, happy, at peace, and unattached everywhere.
MEANING: Ashtavakra is speaking to Janaka, who sits burdened by the weight of a kingdom and the fear of losing it. The wise man tells him that suffering is born of worry and nothing else. He means that the extra pain you feel comes not from the world itself, but from the mind spinning out what might happen next. Once you know that worry is the true source, you can set it down and find calm.
FOR YOU: You check your messages at two in the morning, convinced that a single rating will erase everything. The visa is real, but the fear of what might happen is not. You are awake and terrified, yet the pain you feel is born of worry, and that worry can loosen its grip.

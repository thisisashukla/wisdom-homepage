## Day 1 [hook] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Same person, same mind, same voice. Lose money, and people suddenly treat you as someone else.
TRANSLATION: The same faculties, unimpaired; the same name; the same unclouded mind; the same speech. Yet a man deprived of the warmth of wealth becomes, in a moment, someone else. How strange!
MEANING: Bhartrihari writes to the reader from a quiet place of observation. He speaks of a man who has lost his money. The man still has the same mind, the same voice, the same name. Yet in a moment, the world treats him as a different person. He sees that the warmth of wealth was not his own. It was a borrowed light, and now it has gone cold.
FOR YOU: The ten-minute call took the money, not the person. You still wake at six with the same mind that built twenty-two years at one company. That mind has not changed. The silence after the call is loud, but it is not a verdict on who you are.

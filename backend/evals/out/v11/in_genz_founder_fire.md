## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. The armies are lined up and about to clash. Arjuna is frozen, unsure of his place. Krishna tells him to look at his own nature. He says that doing the work that fits your own nature, even if it is flawed, is better than doing someone else's work perfectly. The work born of your own nature brings no fault. The idea is to offer your own imperfect path as a form of worship.

TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.

The point is that setbacks rarely last for those who keep going on the right path, and the work itself is the reward.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.46
LINE: Doing your own work well can be your worship of the source of all life.
TRANSLATION: By worshipping, through his own work, Him from whom all beings arise and by whom all this is pervaded, a person attains perfection.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that a person reaches perfection by worshipping the source of all life through their own work. He explains that every undertaking has flaws, but doing the work that fits your own nature is the truest offering you can make. It is not about achieving a perfect result, but about giving your own effort back to the life that flows through it.
FOR YOU: Turn inward, come to know the self, and let your work become service and offering; a life lived from there is full.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 2.69
LINE: What the crowd sleeps through, the wise stay awake to. What the crowd chases, the wise see past.
TRANSLATION: What is night for all beings, in that the self-controlled one is awake; and where all beings are awake, that is night for the seeing sage.
MEANING: Krishna is answering Arjuna's question about what a steady mind looks like. He describes a person who is awake when others sleep, and sees clearly when others are blind to what they chase. This is not about physical rest but about guarding where you place your attention. Where you dwell, that is where your inner state is built. Dwelling on what you lack or what angers you is what turns the mind toward ruin. The steady person watches where their attention goes, because that is where the self is formed.
FOR YOU: The Gita is a conversation, not a collection of answers. Let it speak to the quiet in your house. When you sit with it, notice where your attention lands. That quiet is not an empty space. It is the ground where the text can reach you without the noise of the day.

## Day 5 [act] Bhagavad Gita 12.8
LINE: Rest your mind in me, settle your thinking in me, and you will live in me.
TRANSLATION: Fix your mind on me alone, let your understanding settle in me; from then on you will dwell in me. Of this there is no doubt.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna that the way into the divine is not through many difficult steps but through one simple choice. He says to fix your mind on me alone and let your understanding settle there. From that single act of settling, you will truly live in me. It is not about running or doing much. It is about where you place your thoughts. When they stop wandering and rest in one place, the rest follows naturally.

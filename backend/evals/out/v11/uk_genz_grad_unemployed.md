## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Arjuna stands in a chariot, caught between two armies, asking what he must do. Krishna tells him that every duty has flaws, and no path is perfect. The better choice is to walk the path that fits your own nature, even if you stumble. Doing the work born of who you are, without copying another, brings no wrong. It is not about the family name you carry, but about the honest fit of your own hand to the task.

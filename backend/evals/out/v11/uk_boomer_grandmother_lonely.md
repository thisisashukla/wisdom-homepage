## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 10.9
LINE: Their minds on me, they talk of me with each other, and find joy and contentment in it.
TRANSLATION: With minds fixed on me and lives given to me, enlightening one another and always speaking of me, they are content and delighted.
MEANING: Krishna is describing the people who have chosen to walk with him. They do not walk alone. They talk with one another about what they have found, and in that shared talk they light a lamp. The light is not just for one of them. It passes from one to the next, and it clears away the dark. They are content because they are no longer carrying the light by themselves.
FOR YOU: You open the Ramayana in the morning, and the pages give you company. You are not carrying the morning alone, and you are not carrying the Sunday alone. The book is a hand reaching back to you.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is restless, stormy and stubborn, as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna agrees it is difficult to control, but says that practice and letting go can train it. The mind runs, and you cannot force it to stop by force alone. What comes next is that steady practice brings it under your hand, not by fighting it, but by returning to it again and again.

MEANING: Uddalaka Aruni is teaching his son Shvetaketu about sleep. He asks Shvetaketu where a person goes when they sleep. The son cannot answer.

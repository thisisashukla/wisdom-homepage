## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, where Arjuna is frozen by doubt and grief. Krishna sees the danger in Arjuna trying to copy someone else's duty instead of his own. The verse says that your own path, even if you stumble on it, is far safer and better than living someone else's life perfectly. Borrowed lives carry a hidden fear because they are not yours. It is a call to act from your own nature rather than imitating another.
FOR YOU: You are sending your money home and working the night shifts. That is your path, and it is heavy. Your own duty done imperfectly is better than a borrowed life done perfectly.

LINE: Satyakama wanted to study, but first he had to ask his mother a question she could not answer.

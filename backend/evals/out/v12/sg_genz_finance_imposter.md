## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is restless and stubborn, and as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna agrees with this, but he also says that practice and letting go can train it. The verse does not say the mind is bad or that you could force it still. It says the mind is hard to hold, but you can train it with practice and letting go. You feel sick before presenting, and that is a real thing. The verse offers a way to work with that restlessness, not to fight it.
FOR YOU: You overprepare because you are trying to hold a stormy mind. This week, you will practice letting go. When you feel sick before presenting, let that be the moment to stop holding on.

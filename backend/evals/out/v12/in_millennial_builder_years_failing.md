## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.

This is the first time Arjuna admits the struggle out loud. The verse proposes that the mind is naturally hard to control, but it can be trained through steady practice and release. LINE: Every result is uncertain. Some who try succeed and some don't. Those who never try never do.
TRANSLATION: The result of every action is uncertain. Knowing this, some people act and succeed, some do not. But those who never act never succeed at all.
MEANING: Vidula finds her son Sanjaya lying defeated after he has lost his kingdom.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. Arjuna is paralyzed by doubt about his duty to fight. Krishna tells him that it is better to walk your own path imperfectly than to do another's work perfectly. Doing the work that matches your own nature, even if it has flaws, brings no wrong to you.

LINE: As we drop worn-out clothes and put on new ones, the self leaves a worn body for a new one.

## Day 4 [steady] Ashtavakra Gita 15.9
LINE: The body comes and goes; what you truly are does not. Why grieve so?
TRANSLATION: The body, made of nature's qualities, comes, stays and goes. The Self neither comes nor goes. Why grieve for it?
MEANING: Ashtavakra speaks to Janaka, who has stepped back from ruling. The teacher points out that the body is just a collection of qualities that arrives and departs. The Self, what you truly are, does neither. It is the ocean, not the waves. This is why you grieve, because you have mistaken the wave for the whole of you. What you truly are cannot be born and cannot die, so the loss of a form does not touch it.
FOR YOU: You are not the body that aches for your partner, nor the mind that searches for her. You are the awareness in which that search and that love both appear.

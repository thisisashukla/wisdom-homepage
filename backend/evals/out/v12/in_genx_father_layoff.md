## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is restless and stubborn, as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna agrees and says that practice and letting go can train it. This means the mind does not obey you by force. It resists, it wanders, it storms. ## Day 2 [diagnose] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Good fortune the mind clutches becomes trouble. Trouble the mind accepts becomes a kind of fortune.
TRANSLATION: All those dazzling fortunes, if the mind clings to them, are really great calamities in grand disguise. And all those varied misfortunes, if the mind accepts them, are really fortunes.
MEANING: King Janaka, alone on his palace roof after hearing wandering sages, questions everything he has built. He sees that the mind holds the power to turn fortune into disaster and disaster into fortune. A title or a loss is only a circumstance. The mind can clutch at what is gone and make it a ruin, or it can accept the fact of it and walk on. The work of the day does not change. Only the way the mind holds it changes.
FOR YOU: What is real in you cannot be taken or lost; only what changes can be taken, and you are more than what changes.

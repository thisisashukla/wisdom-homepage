## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 11.44
LINE: Bear with me as a father bears with a son, a friend with a friend, a lover with the beloved.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 2.63
LINE: Anger clouds judgement, clouded judgement loses memory, lost memory wrecks reason, and then you fall.
TRANSLATION: From anger comes delusion; from delusion, confusion of memory; from confusion of memory, the ruin of understanding; and with understanding ruined, a person is lost.
MEANING: Krishna is answering Arjuna about what steadiness looks like. He describes a person who is at peace with themselves, not shaken by sorrow, and in control of their senses. Then he traces a clear path: when you keep dwelling on things, it turns into anger. Anger clouds your judgment. Then you lose your memory and your ability to understand. With that understanding gone, you are lost. The steadiness Arjuna asked about is built by guarding where your mind rests. The ruin starts when you let your attention stay on what hurts you.
FOR YOU: The nightly fights start long before the shouting. They start in the quiet minutes you spend turning your father's words over in your head. Today, watch where your mind goes when you are alone. See if it is replaying the fight, building the anger, and clouding the real picture of what is happening.

## Day 4 [steady] Katharudra Upanishad 8
LINE: Don't wear out your body chasing pleasure or fleeing pain. Praised, don't gloat; blamed, don't curse others.
TRANSLATION: Let him not torment the body too much for the sake of pleasure or pain. Praised, let him not gloat; blamed, let him not curse others.

## Day 5 [act] Mahabharata · Vana Parva · Vana: Draupadi and Yudhishthira
LINE: Don't answer anger with anger. The one who stays calm protects both people, and heals both.
TRANSLATION: One who does not get angry back at an angry person saves both himself and the other from great danger. He becomes the healer of both.
MEANING: Yudhishhira is in the forest with Draupadi. She wants him to fight back against the injustice done to them. He tells her that anger only destroys, and forgiveness holds the world together. He says that when someone is angry with you, you do not have to answer with anger. The one who stays calm saves both himself and the other person. They become the healer of both.
FOR YOU: When your father speaks, his words carry his own fear. You do not have to answer that fear with your own anger. You can hear him, and you can still stand on your own ground. That is how you stop the cycle from breaking you both.

## Day 6 [act] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Cut craving, forgive, drop arrogance, speak truth, win over even enemies, be kind to those suffering.
TRANSLATION: Cut off craving, practise forbearance, give up arrogance, take no delight in wrong, speak the truth, follow the path of the good, serve the wise, honour those worthy of honour, win over even your enemies, show courtesy, guard your good name, be kind to those in pain: this is how the good behave.
MEANING: Bhartrihari sits with you, not to tell you to win, but to tell you how to stand. The text says the good behave by cutting craving, forgiving, dropping arrogance, speaking truth, winning over even enemies, and being kind to those suffering.

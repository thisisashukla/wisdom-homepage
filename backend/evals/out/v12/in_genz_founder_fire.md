## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.38
LINE: Hold pleasure and pain, gain and loss, win and defeat as equal. Then step in and act.
TRANSLATION: Treating pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat alike, then engage in the fight. Done this way, you will incur no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, telling him that retreating from the fight will only bring disgrace and endless shame. He says to treat pleasure and pain, gain and loss, victory and defeat as the same thing. Then step in and do what must be done. Done this way, you will not incur any wrong. The idea is to act from a steady place, not from the fear of winning or losing.
FOR YOU: You have left the safety of a job and the money is not here yet. That is exactly what this asks of you. Treat the thrill of starting out and the stress of the empty account as the same. Step in and build your brand anyway, because the work is yours to do.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Effort brings results, sooner or later, depending on time and place. That timing is what we call fate.
TRANSLATION: Results come from effort, sooner or later, depending on place and time. That arrival of the fruit, early or late, is what people call fate.
MEANING: Vasishtha is speaking to Rama. Rama has just learned that his father was killed by his own uncle, and he is sitting in despair. Vasishtha tells him that the future is not a locked door. He says that what people call fate is simply the fruit of their own past effort, arriving early or late. He tells Rama that his own present effort can overcome any past. The effort you put in today is what will shape tomorrow, and you could not waste your energy grieving what was never in your hands.
FOR YOU: You quit your job last week and the money has not come yet. This verse says that the timing of your results is not your fault. Your effort is the only thing that matters, so let it be enough for today.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari spoke this to the reader, sitting at a time when the world feels like it is throwing everything at them. The verse says that let the wise praise or blame. Let money come or go. Let death come today or ages from now. The steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right. The point is that setbacks rarely last for those who keep going on the right path.
FOR YOU: The morning comes and the work feels heavy. The praise from strangers has not arrived. Let the money come or go. You do not need to step off the path you chose. Just keep going.

## Day 4 [steady] Mahabharata · Vana Parva · Vana: Draupadi and Yudhishthira
LINE: Don't despair because effort hasn't paid off yet. Every attempt can go either way.
TRANSLATION: 'I keep working and still don't succeed' is no reason to despair. Every effort can end two ways, in success or not; only by not acting is the outcome certain.
MEANING: In the forest, Draupadi speaks to Yudhishthira. She is tired of watching the good suffer while waiting for fate to fix things. She says that simply waiting for a reward is a losing game. She tells him to stop worrying about results and just do what is right, while working as if the whole future depends on his own hands.

## Day 5 [act] Bhagavad Gita 2.47
LINE: Your work is yours to do; the results are not yours to hold. Don't let that make you stop.
TRANSLATION: Your right is to the work alone, never to its fruits. Do not let the fruit be your motive, and do not cling to doing nothing.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, seeing him hesitate. He tells him that his duty is to act, to fight, without being attached to whether he wins or loses. The right to act belongs to him alone. The outcome is not in his hands. Do the work with full attention, but let go of the need to control the result.
FOR YOU: The work is building your brand. The result is still out of reach. Do the work today as if the result does not exist, and let the work itself be the reward.

## Day 6 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
MEANING: Vidura speaks to King Dhritarashtra, who lies awake in the dark before the peace talks. He tells the king that a wise person decides calmly, acts steadily, and does not stop halfway. They do not waste time or lose their grip on themselves. The point is that real happiness comes from clear action, not from waiting for the right moment.
FOR YOU: You decided to leave the job and you began. Now the money has not come and the morning alarm feels heavy. Do not let that heaviness make you stop. Decide again, this morning, that the work is yours, and begin the small task in front of you without pausing halfway.

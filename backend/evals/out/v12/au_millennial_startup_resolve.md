## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.40
LINE: No sincere effort here is ever wasted. Even a little of it shields you from great fear.
TRANSLATION: On this path no effort is lost and no harm results. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, right before the great war. Arjuna is paralyzed by doubt, and Krishna is telling him to act. Krishna says that on this path of action, no sincere effort is ever wasted. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear. The idea is simple: do the work fully, and let go of owning the result.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna that a person who gives up the fruit of action finds lasting peace. The one who stays attached to the result is bound. The disciplined person acts without being stained by the outcome, like a lotus leaf untouched by water. What comes next in the story is that this freedom is the mark of the wise.
FOR YOU: You signed the customer yesterday. That win is real. But the real test is the quiet month ahead when no one is watching. Let the result be what it is. Do the work without letting the high or the doubt take root.

MEANING: Bhartrihari speaks to the reader from his text on steadfastness. He says the gods churned the ocean through jewels and poison until they found nectar. ## Day 6 [act] Bhagavad Gita 3.19
LINE: So keep doing the work that needs doing, well and without clinging. That's how you rise.
TRANSLATION: Therefore, always do the work that has to be done, without attachment; by working without attachment a person reaches the highest.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on a battlefield, asking why the fulfilled would still act. He says even the wise, even God, work for the sake of the world, and the one content in the self has nothing left to gain. Yet the wise keep working, like Janaka, because others follow their example. The verse proposes that you could always do the work that needs doing, well and without clinging, and that is how you rise.
FOR YOU: Rise and act; do your work fully and well each day, let the result go, and keep at it when no one is watching.

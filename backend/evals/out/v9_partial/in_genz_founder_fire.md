## Day 1 [hook] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidula's counsel
LINE: Blaze like a torch, even for a moment. Don't just smoulder to stay alive.
TRANSLATION: Like a torch of tinduka wood, blaze, even if only for a moment. Do not smoulder without flame like a fire of husks, just to stay alive.
MEANING: Queen Vidula finds her son Sanjaya lying defeated after losing his kingdom. She tells him to blaze like a torch, even for a moment. She says do not smoulder without flame like a fire of husks, just to stay alive. She means past failure does not define you. Results are uncertain, but those who never try never succeed.
FOR YOU: You quit your job last week and feel alive. That fire is yours. Let it burn bright in the early days, even if the work is small. Blaze like a torch.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
FOR YOU: The work of building your brand is yours to do. Let the worry about the next paycheck be the water, and let your effort be the leaf. Do the next right thing for the work itself, and let the result be what it will.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari is speaking to a reader who is caught between what others think and what they know is right. The world is loud with praise and blame, with money arriving and leaving. The verse tells us to let all of that happen without stepping off the path. It says the steady person does not move a step from what is right, whether fortune smiles or turns away, whether death comes today or in ages. It is a picture of staying put when everything around you is moving. The steady one keeps going.
FOR YOU: You left a job to build something that is yours. The praise and the doubt will come. Let them come. Your path is the work itself, not the money or the approval. When the first hard month arrives, let it arrive. Do not step off the path.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 2.40
LINE: No sincere effort here is ever wasted. Even a little of it shields you from great fear.
TRANSLATION: On this path no effort is lost and no harm results. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, where the work is heavy and the outcome is unknown. He tells him that no sincere effort on this path is ever lost. Even a little of this practice protects one from great fear. The idea is to do the work fully and let go of owning the result.
FOR YOU: You quit your job last week. The first hard month of no income will test the fire you feel right now. Your part is to keep building the brand, to let the work be enough even when the results are far away.

## Day 5 [act] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
MEANING: Vidura tells Dhritarashtra that the wise person decides and then begins, does not stop halfway, does not waste time, and keeps themselves in hand. This person is called wise. The wise person decides calmly, acts steadily, forgives freely, and knows what real happiness is made of.
FOR YOU: You cannot tell if continuing is discipline or stubbornness. Pick one small task for your brand today and do it without stopping. Let the steady work be the proof that you are wise enough to build your own thing.

## Day 7 [story] Hanuman's leap
LINE: Halfway across the ocean, a golden mountain rose out of the water and offered him a place to rest.

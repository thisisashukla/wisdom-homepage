LINE: As we drop worn-out clothes and put on new ones, the self leaves a worn body for a new one.

MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, just after Arjuna has laid down his bow and declared he will not fight. He is grieving the men he must kill, and he is lost. ## Day 4 [steady] Ashtavakra Gita 15.9
LINE: The body comes and goes; what you truly are does not. Why grieve so?
TRANSLATION: The body, made of nature's qualities, comes, stays and goes. The Self neither comes nor goes. Why grieve for it?
MEANING: Ashtavakra speaks to Janaka. He points out that the body is born of nature, it appears, it stays for a time, and then it goes. The Self, the true awareness inside, does not come and it does not go. It is always there. Why then should you grieve for it? He is telling Janaka that he is not the changing body or the mind's passing thoughts, but the steady ocean in which all of life's waves rise and fall.

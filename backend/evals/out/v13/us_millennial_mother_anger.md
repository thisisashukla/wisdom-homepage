## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.63
LINE: Anger clouds judgement, clouded judgement loses memory, lost memory wrecks reason, and then you fall.
TRANSLATION: From anger comes delusion; from delusion, confusion of memory; from confusion of memory, the ruin of understanding; and with understanding ruined, a person is lost.
MEANING: Arjuna asks Krishna what a person of steady mind looks like. Krishna tells him that when you dwell on what angers you, anger clouds your judgement. Once judgement is clouded, memory falters, and with it, your understanding of what is right and wrong. That is when you fall. The verse says that steadiness is built by guarding what you give your attention to. Anger is not the first step. It is what comes after you have already let your mind sit on a wound.
FOR YOU: You manage twelve people at work without a raised voice. At home, the same mind snaps at your seven-year-old. The split is not who you are.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 3.37
LINE: It's desire, which turns into anger when blocked. Never satisfied, it is the real enemy here.
TRANSLATION: The Blessed Lord said: It is desire, it is anger, born of the quality of passion; all-devouring and greatly harmful. Know this to be the enemy here.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna after he asks what force drives a person to commit wrong against their own will. He says that desire is the root cause. When a desire is blocked or unfulfilled, it becomes anger. This anger is born of passion and is like a fire that consumes everything. It is the true enemy that clouds the mind and leads to ruin.
FOR YOU: At work, desire is met and things run. At home, the quiet is broken, and that unmet need for rest turns into the snap. The anger is not your nature. It is a signal that the mind has no place to land. Let it be a signal, not a verdict on who you are.

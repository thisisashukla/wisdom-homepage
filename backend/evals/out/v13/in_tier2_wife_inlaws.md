LINE: Growth isn't size. A small tree bearing fruit has grown more than a tall, empty one.
TRANSLATION: Growth isn't measured by the size of the body. A short tree that bears fruit has grown; a tall one that bears none has not.
MEANING: Twelve-year-old Ashtavakra stands at the gate of King Janaka’s court. The gatekeeper blocks his path because he is only a boy. Ashtavakra argues that age and size do not measure wisdom. A short tree that bears fruit has grown more than a tall, empty one. What he offers is the truth underneath the insult: the gatekeeper is looking at a body, not a mind. LINE: Speak words that don't wound, that are true, kind and helpful. That is discipline of speech.
TRANSLATION: Speech that causes no distress, that is truthful, pleasant and beneficial, and the regular study of sacred texts: this is called austerity of speech.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Mahabharata · Shanti Parva · Shanti: Consolation of Yudhishthira
LINE: In grief, ask: where did I come from, where am I going, who am I? Let that steady you.
TRANSLATION: 'Where was I before? Where will I go? Who am I, really, and why am I here? Why should I grieve, and for what?' Ask these, and steady the mind.
MEANING: King Janaka asks the sage Ashma how to bear the loss of those he loved and the wealth that once surrounded him. Ashma says that pain comes from wanting what passes away, and that days do not return. He asks Janaka to steady his mind with three questions. Where did I come from. Where will I go. Who am I, really. These questions are not a way to escape grief. They are a way to hold it steady so you can stand in the world that is still here.

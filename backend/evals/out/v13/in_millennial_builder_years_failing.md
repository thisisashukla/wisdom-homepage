## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.37
LINE: What happens to someone who believes, tries, slips, and doesn't make it to the end?
TRANSLATION: Arjuna said: One who has faith but does not strive enough, whose mind falls away from yoga before reaching perfection, what end does he come to, Krishna?
MEANING: Arjuna asks Krishna: what becomes of someone who believes and tries, but slips before reaching the end? Krishna answers that no sincere effort is ever lost. The path does not close behind them. The work they have already done stays with them, and they simply begin again from where they stopped, without starting over.
FOR YOU: You have been building these apps for years. You do not know what is wrong. This verse says that trying and stopping is not the same as failing. The years you have already spent are not wasted. They stay with you.

## Day 3 [steady] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: A mind opened by clear thinking doesn't sink in hard times. It floats, like a gourd on water.
TRANSLATION: Minds that have been opened by discernment do not sink in misfortune, just as gourds do not sink in water.
MEANING: Vasishtha speaks to Rama after he has asked about the four gatekeepers that guard the door to freedom. He explains that a mind opened by clear thinking will not sink when misfortune comes. It stays afloat, like a gourd on water. The point is that you only need to begin with one gatekeeper, such as calm or good company, and the rest will strengthen themselves.
FOR YOU: You are holding your work and your children and your parents, and still you are trying to build. That is not nothing. Even a small hold on something steady, like the effort itself, keeps you from sinking. Trust that this much is enough for today.

## Day 5 [act] Valmiki Ramayana · Sundara Kanda
LINE: Refusing to lose heart is the root of success. Look again where you have not yet looked.
TRANSLATION: Not losing heart is the root of success; not losing heart is the highest happiness. I will search again, in the places I have not yet searched.
MEANING: Hanuman has searched all of Lanka through the night and cannot find Sita. Despair weighs on him, and he considers what failure will mean for Rama and the others.

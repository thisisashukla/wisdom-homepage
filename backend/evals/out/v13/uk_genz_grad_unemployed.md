## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Arjuna stands in the middle of a field, unsure, watching the lives of others. Krishna tells him that the work born of your own nature is the only work that will not eat you alive. He says it is better to walk your own path with all its cracks than to perform another's role with perfect polish. The truth is that every path has flaws, and your only job is to do the work that fits you, flaws and all, as an offering.
FOR YOU: The applications you sent out are your own path, even if the replies have not come. The work of making flat whites is your work, done with your own hands. Do that work today, not as a step down, but as the honest offering it is.

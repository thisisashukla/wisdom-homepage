## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield. Arjuna is frozen, caught between two armies of family and duty. Krishna tells him that it is better to follow the path that is truly yours, even if you stumble, than to perform someone else's role with perfection. The idea is that your own nature holds a truth that fits you alone. When you act from that honest fit, you carry no wrong.
FOR YOU: You are still going home for Diwali, sitting at your own table, but the silence tells you they are asking you to perform a role that is not yours. Let your own truth be the offering at the table today, even if it feels imperfect.

LINE: Free of clinging and boasting, full of resolve and enthusiasm, unchanged by success or failure: the best kind of doer.
TRANSLATION: The doer who is free from attachment, does not speak of himself, is full of steadiness and enthusiasm, and unmoved by success or failure, is called sattvic.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, laying out the three ways a person can act based on their inner nature. He describes the best kind of doer, the one who is free of clinging, does not boast, and is full of steady enthusiasm. This person remains unchanged by success or failure, and is called sattvic. The best joy is poison at first and nectar later.

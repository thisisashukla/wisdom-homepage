## Day 1 [hook] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Some never start for fear of obstacles; some quit when obstacles hit. The best keep going.
TRANSLATION: The lowest do not begin at all, for fear of obstacles. The middling begin, but stop when obstacles strike. The best, struck by obstacles again and again, do not give up what they have begun.
MEANING: Bhartrihari speaks to you from his text on the value of learning, describing the three kinds of people who meet obstacles. The lowest never begin, fearing what might go wrong. The middling start, but stop when trouble arrives. The best, struck by obstacles again and again, do not give up what they have begun. The point is that what you learn and how you speak are wealth no one can take from you. The truth underneath is that starting and staying are different, and the real failure is not the obstacle but the stopping.
FOR YOU: You have been going for years with this side project, and you no longer know if you are stuck in discipline or in stubbornness. You do not need to know the whole path today.

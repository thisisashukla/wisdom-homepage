## Day 1 [hook] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidula's counsel
LINE: Blaze like a torch, even for a moment. Don't just smoulder to stay alive.
TRANSLATION: Like a torch of tinduka wood, blaze, even if only for a moment. Do not smoulder without flame like a fire of husks, just to stay alive.
MEANING: Queen Vidula finds her son Sanjaya lying defeated after losing his kingdom. She tells him to blaze like a torch of tinduka wood, even for a moment. She says do not smoulder without flame like a fire of husks, just to stay alive. She wants him to rise and fight, not to linger in despair.
FOR YOU: You felt alive the moment you quit your job. That is your own fire, not just the relief of leaving. Let it burn bright for the work itself, not as proof that you are brave.

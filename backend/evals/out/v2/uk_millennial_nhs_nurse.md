## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna while the armies stand ready, and he names the weight of doing what belongs to you. He says that your own duty, walked with all its flaws, is better than another's done perfectly. You do not fail by doing what fits you. You incur no wrong by living out your own nature.

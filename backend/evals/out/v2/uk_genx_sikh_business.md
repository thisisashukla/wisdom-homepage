## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.7
LINE: I'm confused about what's right. Tell me plainly what's best; I'm ready to learn.
TRANSLATION: My nature is stricken by weakness and my mind is confused about what is right. I ask you: tell me for certain what is best for me. I am your student; I have come to you, teach me.
MEANING: Arjuna stands on the battlefield, his body trembling, his mind a blur of grief and doubt. He looks at Krishna and admits he is paralyzed, his nature stricken by weakness. He asks Krishna to tell him plainly what is best, to teach him like a student who has come to his master for clarity.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Affection fades with distance, friendship without warmth, wealth with carelessness, a child with spoiling.
TRANSLATION: A king is ruined by bad counsel, an ascetic by attachment, a son by spoiling, a brahmin by neglecting study, a family by a wayward son, character by serving the wicked, modesty by drink, farming by neglect, affection by living far away, friendship by lack of warmth, prosperity by bad conduct, and wealth by careless spending.
MEANING: Bhartrihari is speaking to you from a quiet corner, looking at how things slip away. He says that a king falls because of bad advice, a family crumbles because of a wayward child, and wealth vanishes through careless spending. He is not saying you are careless. He is showing you the pattern. The world watches what you have, and it changes how it treats you. Your father sees the business, not the years you spent building it. The only real loss is if you let his small view of you become your own.
FOR YOU: He sees a ledger, not a life. When he walks in and changes a decision, he is not managing your work. He is spending the capital of your respect. Your job today is to see that his entrance is his smallness, not your failure.

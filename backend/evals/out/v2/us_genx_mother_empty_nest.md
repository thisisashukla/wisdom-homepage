## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.27
LINE: Whatever is born will die, and what dies is born again. Don't torment yourself over the unavoidable.
TRANSLATION: Death is certain for the one who is born, and birth is certain for the one who dies. So you should not grieve over what cannot be avoided.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, where Arjuna is weeping over the men he must fight and the family he will destroy. He tells Arjuna that the self is never born and never dies, and that what is born must die, and what dies must be born again. He asks Arjuna not to torment himself over what is unavoidable, because the truth of who you are outlasts every passing moment and every loss.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is as hard to control as the wind. It is turbulent and stubborn, and he feels he cannot hold it steady. Krishna agrees that the mind is restless, but he says that through practice and letting go, it can be trained. The mind is not meant to be forced into stillness. It is meant to be guided, again and again, until it learns where to settle.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: Worry grows when you feed it thought, like fire with fuel. Starve it, and it dies down.
TRANSLATION: Worry grows by thinking, as fire grows with fuel; and it dies by not being thought, as fire dies without fuel.
MEANING: Vasishtha is speaking to Rama after the story of two brothers who lost their parents. The elder brother, Punya, saw that the younger, Pavana, was drowning in grief. Vasishtha tells Rama that worry grows by thinking, just as a fire grows with fuel, and it dies when you stop feeding it. The mind is not a solid thing. It is a habit of returning to the same thoughts. You can stop adding wood to the fire.
FOR YOU: Your mind is adding wood to a fire that has not happened. Today, when you catch yourself rehearsing a fear, do not argue with it. Simply stop adding the next thought. Let the flame use up what is already there and go out.

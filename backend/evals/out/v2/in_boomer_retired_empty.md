## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.69
LINE: What the crowd sleeps through, the wise stay awake to. What the crowd chases, the wise see past.
TRANSLATION: What is night for all beings, in that the self-controlled one is awake; and where all beings are awake, that is night for the seeing sage.
MEANING: Arjuna asks what a steady mind looks like. Krishna answers that a steady person is awake where others sleep, and sees clearly what others chase. He says the self-controlled are awake in the night of all beings, and where all beings are awake, that is night to the seeing sage. The point is that steadiness is built by guarding attention, and anger starts with what you dwell on.

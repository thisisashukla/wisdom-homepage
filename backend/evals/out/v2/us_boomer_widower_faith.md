## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.22
LINE: As we drop worn-out clothes and put on new ones, the self leaves a worn body for a new one.
TRANSLATION: Just as a person casts off worn-out clothes and puts on new ones, so the embodied self casts off worn-out bodies and enters others that are new.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the field of battle, when Arjuna has dropped his bow and said he will not fight. He has just told Arjuna that the self is eternal, untouched by weapons, fire, water or wind. Now he speaks this verse to show that death is only a change of body, not a loss of who we are. The self simply leaves one worn-out body and enters another, just as we leave old clothes for new.
FOR YOU: The evenings are hard because the busyness stops and the question of where she is now has no answer yet. What this verse says is that what is real in you and in her cannot be lost. It does not answer the question, but it names the truth underneath it.

## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 2.8
LINE: Even winning everything wouldn't touch this grief that's drying me up inside.
TRANSLATION: I see nothing that could drive away this sorrow that is drying up my senses, not even an unrivalled, prosperous kingdom on earth or rule over the gods.
MEANING: Arjuna stands on the battlefield and tells Krishna he sees no way to lift the grief that is drying him up inside. He says that even an unrivalled kingdom on earth or rule over the gods would not touch this sorrow. He is asking what is right, because his own mind is lost. Admitting you do not know and asking for help is where clarity begins. It is the first step toward seeing what is truly his own.

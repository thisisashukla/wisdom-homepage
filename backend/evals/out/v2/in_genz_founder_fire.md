## Day 1 [hook] Mahabharata · Udyoga Parva · Vidura Niti
LINE: Decide, then begin. Don't stall halfway, don't waste time, and keep yourself steady.
TRANSLATION: One who begins only after deciding, does not stop halfway through the work, does not waste time and keeps himself in hand, is called wise.
MEANING: Vidura speaks to the sleepless King Dhritarashtra the night before a great peace talk. He says that a wise person first decides calmly, then begins the work, does not stop halfway, wastes no time, and keeps a steady hand. This is not about rushing. It is about choosing your path clearly, and then walking it without looking back or pausing to doubt.
FOR YOU: You left your job and chose your own path. That choice is already made. Now you keep your hand steady on the wheel. Do not look back at the old life. Just keep going.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, right before the war begins. Arjuna is torn, looking at the family he must fight. Krishna is not telling him to abandon his duty. He is telling him to act without letting the outcome own his mind. The disciplined person acts, gives up the fruit, and finds peace. The undisciplined person is bound by desire for the result.
FOR YOU: You are waiting for the brand to pay off and for people to notice it. That prize is not here yet. Let it go. Do the work for the work itself. The peace you feel will stay with you even when the money is gone.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari writes to the reader about steadfastness. He says let people praise or blame you. Let money come or go. Let anything happen. The steady person does not step off the right path. They keep going no matter what the world says or what the future brings.
FOR YOU: People will say you are foolish for quitting your job. Let them. The work you are doing now is the right path. Keep going. Your resolve is enough. The work itself is the reward.

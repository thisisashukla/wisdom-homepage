LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the field. He tells him to take up his own path, even if it is flawed, rather than copying another person's duties perfectly. Doing the work that fits your own nature brings no guilt. It is an offering of your true self.

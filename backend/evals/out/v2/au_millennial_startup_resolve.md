## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.26
LINE: Free of clinging and boasting, full of resolve and enthusiasm, unchanged by success or failure: the best kind of doer.
TRANSLATION: The doer who is free from attachment, does not speak of himself, is full of steadiness and enthusiasm, and unmoved by success or failure, is called sattvic.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, sorting what drives a person. He describes the best kind of doer: someone free from clinging to results and free from boasting. This person acts with steadiness and enthusiasm, unchanged by success or failure. The joy they take is hard at first and sweet later.
FOR YOU: You signed the first customer yesterday. The high is real, and it is yours. But the year ahead will test you when the morning work is just work. Stay in the doing, not the result.

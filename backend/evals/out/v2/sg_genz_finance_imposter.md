## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.14
LINE: Every result has five causes: the setting, the doer, the tools, the efforts, and the unseen. You're one of them.
TRANSLATION: The seat, the doer, the various instruments, the many kinds of effort, and fifth, providence: these are the five causes of every action.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna about the five roots behind every action. There is the setting, the person acting, the tools they use, the effort they put in, and the unseen hand. You are only one of those five. This means you own your part in what happens, but you do not own the whole outcome.
FOR YOU: When you feel sick before a presentation, you are treating yourself as the only cause of the result. You are carrying the weight of the setting, the tools, the effort, and the unseen, all by yourself. Let the room, the topic, and the audience be the other four causes.

TRANSLATION: A person should never look down on himself. The one who despises himself never finds real prosperity.
MEANING: Draupadi tells Yudhishthira that a person should never look down on themselves. The one who despises themselves never finds real prosperity. She argues that waiting on fate is no plan, and that effort still matters.
FOR YOU: The verdict you pass on yourself is the real enemy in that room. It is not your background that makes the air feel dangerous. It is the story you tell yourself about not belonging. The text says self-contempt never leads to good. Let that be the pattern you see today.

LINE: Don't think less of yourself or settle for little. Set your mind on something good, and stop fearing.

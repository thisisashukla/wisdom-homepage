## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 6.34
LINE: The mind is restless, stormy, stubborn and strong. Holding it feels like holding the wind.
TRANSLATION: For the mind is restless, Krishna, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. I think it is as hard to hold as the wind.
MEANING: Arjuna tells Krishna that the mind is restless, turbulent, powerful and stubborn. He says it feels as hard to hold as the wind. Krishna does not deny this. He simply says that practice and letting go can train it.
FOR YOU: You are holding the wind right now. The guilt and the split are not failures. They are the mind in its natural storm. Let them be there without trying to pin them down.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Yoga Vasishtha
LINE: No one hurts us like we hurt ourselves. The mind becomes the mind's own enemy.
TRANSLATION: Our own senses are the enemies; truth itself has turned untrue; the self strikes the self, and the mind is the mind's own enemy.

LINE: A ball knocked down bounces back up. For good people, setbacks rarely last.
TRANSLATION: A ball struck down by the hand flies right back up. As a rule, the misfortunes of those who live rightly do not last.
MEANING: Bhartrihari is talking about what it means to stay steady. He says a ball knocked down by a hand flies right back up. ## Day 4 [steady] Ramcharitmanas · Uttar Kand
LINE: A mother has the boil lanced though the child cries. Some humbling is like that.
TRANSLATION: So the merciful one removes it, for he loves his servant deeply. When a boil forms on a child's body, the mother has it lanced as if she were hard-hearted. Though the child suffers at first and cries out, the mother, to cure the illness, pays no heed to that pain.
MEANING: Kakbhushundi, the devoted crow-sage, once watched the child Ram in Ayodhya. He doubted that a toddler chasing a crow could be God, and Ram reached for him across every world, showing him universes inside a child's mouth. Humbled, he was offered every power and chose devotion alone. In that moment, Ram explained that he lances the boils of pride from those he loves, just as a mother lances a painful boil on her child. She pays no heed to the crying because she is curing the illness.
FOR YOU: Your daughter's question opened a sore you already knew was there. You do not have to choose between being her mother and being a manager. In the moments you have with her, be fully there. At work, let the project be fully yours. The love you give does not shrink when your hands are busy.

LINE: Be on your own side. Lift yourself up, don't push yourself down. You can be your own friend.
TRANSLATION: Raise yourself by your own self; do not let yourself sink. For the self alone is the friend of the self, and the self alone is the enemy of the self.

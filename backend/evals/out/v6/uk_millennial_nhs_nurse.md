## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield. Arjuna is frozen by grief and confusion, unable to see past the rules of family and status. Krishna points to the nature of duty itself, saying that every path has flaws. The work that is truly yours, done with honest effort, is better than another's path done perfectly but without your own truth in it. It is about finding your fit and doing it as an offering, flaws and all.
FOR YOU: After a long night shift, your mind replays the call. Your own path is the work you do and the money you send home, not the marriage clock they hang up on you.

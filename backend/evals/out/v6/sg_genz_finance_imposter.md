## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.48
LINE: Don't abandon work that suits you just because it has flaws. Every undertaking has some, like fire has smoke.
TRANSLATION: One should not give up the work suited to one's nature, Kaunteya, even if it has faults; for all undertakings are clouded by faults, as fire is by smoke.
MEANING: Krishna tells Arjuna not to abandon the work that fits his nature just because it is flawed. He says every undertaking has its faults, just as fire has smoke. The proposal is to do the work that suits you, with all its imperfections, as an offering.
FOR YOU: The sickness you feel before presenting is not a sign you are wrong. It is the smoke of the fire you are asked to tend. Do the work that fits you, with all its flaws.

## Day 4 [steady] Bhagavad Gita 2.57
LINE: Good news doesn't inflate them, bad news doesn't embitter them. That's steady wisdom.
TRANSLATION: One who is without attachment anywhere, who neither rejoices on meeting good nor hates on meeting bad, has wisdom that is firmly set.

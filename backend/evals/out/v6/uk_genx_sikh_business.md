## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 3.35
LINE: Better your own path done imperfectly than someone else's done perfectly. Borrowed lives are dangerous.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, even done imperfectly, than the duty of another well done. Better is death in one's own duty; the duty of another is full of fear.
MEANING: Krishna speaks to Arjuna on the battlefield, telling him to act according to his own nature rather than imitate another's. He says that following your own path, even if done imperfectly, is better than performing the duty of another perfectly. Borrowing a life that is not yours brings only fear, while death in your own duty is noble. The idea is that living out your own truth is safer and more authentic than wearing a mask that fits someone else.
FOR YOU: You run the business, but your father walks in and changes decisions in front of your staff. That is not your duty. This verse says that living out your own calling, even when it is messy, is better than performing a role that is not yours.

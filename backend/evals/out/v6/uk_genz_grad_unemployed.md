## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Krishna is telling Arjuna that it is better to do your own work, even if it is imperfect, than to do someone else's work perfectly. He says that doing the work that fits your own nature means you do not incur any wrong. The point is to do what matches who you are, flaws and all, and offer it up.

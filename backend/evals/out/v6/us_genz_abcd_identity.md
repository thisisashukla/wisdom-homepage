## Day 1 [hook] Bhagavad Gita 18.47
LINE: Better your own path, imperfectly walked, than someone else's done perfectly.
TRANSLATION: Better is one's own duty, though imperfect, than the duty of another well performed. Doing the work born of one's own nature, one incurs no wrong.
MEANING: Arjuna is frozen on the battlefield, and Krishna is telling him to stop measuring his worth against some impossible standard. The idea is that your own work, done honestly in your own nature, is enough. It is an offering. You do not need to perform perfection for them. The work that fits your own nature, with all its flaws, is the only work that truly belongs to you.
FOR YOU: You are being asked to become a version of yourself that fits their picture, not your size. The anger you feel is the right response. Stay with the work that is actually yours, the studying and the living that is already in your hands.

## Day 2 [diagnose] Ramcharitmanas · Sundar Kand
LINE: When advisers, doctors and teachers only say what pleases you, the ruin comes quickly.
TRANSLATION: When a minister, a doctor and a teacher speak only to please, out of fear or hope of reward, then the kingdom, the body and righteousness are soon destroyed.
MEANING: In the Ramcharitmanas, Ravana sits in his court while his ministers tell him only what he wants to hear. Tulsidas points out that when the people around you only say what pleases you, ruin comes quickly. The truth is hidden in the room. The problem is not the truth itself, but the circle that keeps you from it.
FOR YOU: You are in a room where the only sound is what they want to hear. The fight you are in is the sound of truth trying to get in. Notice who is in that room with you.

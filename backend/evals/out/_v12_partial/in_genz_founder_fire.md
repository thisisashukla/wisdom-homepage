## Day 2 [diagnose] Bhagavad Gita 5.12
LINE: Let go of the payoff and you find lasting peace. Chase it and it chains you.
TRANSLATION: The disciplined person, giving up the fruit of action, attains lasting peace. The undisciplined, attached to the fruit and driven by desire, is bound.
MEANING: Krishna is speaking to Arjuna on the battlefield, where everything feels heavy and final. He is telling him that the person who acts without clinging to the result finds a deep, steady peace. The person who is desperate for the prize is trapped by it. The idea is to be fully inside the work without letting the mess soak into you. It is not about refusing a fair wage, but about not being chained to the outcome.
FOR YOU: You left with no income and you are building something that is yours. The fire you feel now is real, but the next months will ask you to work without the promise of a quick reward. Try to stay in the work itself, and let the result find you later.

## Day 3 [steady] Bhartrihari · Niti Shatakam
LINE: Let them praise or blame, let money come or go: the steady don't step off the right path.
TRANSLATION: Let the worldly-wise blame or praise; let fortune come or go as she pleases; let death come today or ages hence: the steadfast do not move a step from the path of what is right.
MEANING: Bhartrihari speaks to the reader from his quiet study, knowing that praise and blame are just noise. He says that fortune and even death may come today or ages from now, but the steady person does not step off the right path. The verse proposes that when you stay on the path, setbacks are short. They do not last for those who keep going.
FOR YOU: Your brand is your path, and the silence from the world is just noise. The hard months will come, but they will not last if you keep going. Hold to the work itself and let the rest of the noise pass through.

## Day 4 [steady] Mahabharata · Vana Parva · Vana: Draupadi and Yudhishthira
LINE: Don't despair because effort hasn't paid off yet. Every attempt can go either way.
TRANSLATION: 'I keep working and still don't succeed' is no reason to despair. Every effort can end two ways, in success or not; only by not acting is the outcome certain.
MEANING: Draupadi speaks to Yudhishthira in the forest. She sees him waiting for fate to fix their suffering. She says that working hard and not seeing results yet is not a reason to stop. Every attempt can end in success or in failure. The only way to be certain of a bad outcome is to do nothing at all. She tells him to do what is right without expecting a reward, and then to keep working as if everything depends on his own action.
FOR YOU: You are living in the hard part now. The steady work you do today is the path itself. Keep going, even when the results are still coming.

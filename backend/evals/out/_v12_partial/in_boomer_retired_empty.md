LINE: What the crowd sleeps through, the wise stay awake to. What the crowd chases, the wise see past.
TRANSLATION: What is night for all beings, in that the self-controlled one is awake; and where all beings are awake, that is night for the seeing sage.
MEANING: Krishna is answering Arjuna about what a steady person looks like, and he is drawing a line between two kinds of night. One is the night when everyone else is asleep, and the wise are awake.
